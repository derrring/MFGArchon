- **AGENTS.md: a PR stays at its plan** (user ruling, 2026-10-25), a new subsection after § *Local patches are the
  anti-pattern*.
  - The approved plan fixes a PR's scope, as its class line fixes its population. A finding outside both goes through
    *Before filing an issue*, and the work continues on the plan; its author does not widen the PR.
  - A finding is structural when it falsifies the plan's premise or acceptance check, concerns a convention or owner
    several implementations share, is a default-path wrong result in code the PR already changes, or changes a #2512
    phase denominator. Its author sends it to the plan's approver in one line and keeps working the plan; the approver
    rules whether to widen, split or defer, and a widening is recorded beside the plan's approval.
