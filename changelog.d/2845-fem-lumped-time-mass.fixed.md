- **P1 FEM lumps the mass in its time derivative, HJB-FEM and FP-FEM together** (Issue #2845). With the consistent mass, FP-FEM's implicit pure-diffusion step went negative at small dt (on a one-node spike, h = 1/32, from dt = 1e-3 down), and the positivity clip after it injected 1.2e-2 to 6.3e-2 of the mass per step. The row-sum lumped mass makes that step an M-matrix step wherever every off-diagonal of the stiffness is <= 0: on P1 in 1-D always; in 2-D when each interior edge's opposite angles sum to at most pi and each boundary edge's opposite angle is at most pi/2; in 3-D a Delaunay mesh does not give it. The row sums are kept, so the mass a solve measures is unchanged. P2, the tensor-product elements and meshless Galerkin keep the consistent mass and solve bit for bit as before; the source, the Hamiltonian's mass and the Newton term stay consistent. An advection-dominated FEM step can still go negative, and the clip still decides it.
