- **`Model(terminal_coupling=...)` is bound and solved** (#1642, stage 2). It was refused, and before that accepted and read by nothing.
  - It takes the specifications `coupling_cost` takes: `InteractionCost(kernel)`, `PotentialCost(V)`, their sum, or a part of the user's own whose `bind(problem)` returns an `EnergyFunctional`. `MFGProblem` binds it at construction as `problem.terminal_energy`, and again on a rebuild.
  - The terminal condition is u(T, x) = g(x) + δG/δm at m(T). Its one owner is `MFGProblem.terminal_value(m_T)`, and every HJB solve is given it at the density of that same solve: each sweep of the grid couplers, each node of `GraphMFGSolver` and each regime of `RegimeSwitchingIterator`, each warm-up step and each residual evaluation of `NewtonMFGSolver`, and `BaseHJBSolver.solve` at its frozen uniform density. The re-imposition after each update and the restore after a diverged step read the same value.
  - **Refused by name:** a `terminal_coupling` that is not a coupling cost, such as a plain `G(x, m)`; G on a network, whose message names `NetworkMFGComponents(terminal_node_value_func=...)` (#2568); G in a `MultiPopulationIterator`, which with more than one population couples them through their stacked densities; FEM and meshless geometries, until stage 1b.
  - The terminal-value cascades in `FixedPointIterator`, `FictitiousPlayIterator`, `BlockIterator` and `MFGResidual` are deleted.
