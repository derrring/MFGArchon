- **A Hamiltonian whose doors read another Hamiltonian than its methods, or that drops the soft wall, is refused at construction** (#2901, stage 1c-ii). The solvers read a Hamiltonian's doors, `evaluate_H`, `evaluate_dp` and `evaluate_optimal_control`, after the soft wall is composed, and before this no check compared a door with its method, read the control's door, or read the wall back: a door overridden to read another Hamiltonian, or one written without the potential, solved a different problem with no error. Construction now compares each door with its method on the composed Hamiltonian, in the forms its readers hand it, at t = 0, T/2 and T: `evaluate_H` and `evaluate_dp` on batches of the probe rows, two or more, and at one point, as HJB-SL's `_sl_value_update` reads them, and `evaluate_optimal_control` on batches alone, as its one reader, `compute_fp_velocity_field`, reads it. The rows are at the consistency check's probes plus the node where the wall is largest (docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md, "What construction compares"), and `validate_composed_wall` holds the walled `__call__` to minus the wall's change of the potential. A door that raises where its method reads is refused, naming the reader that hands it that form, except on a batch for a Hamiltonian that declares `reads_a_batch` False, whose batch paths raise at solve time; such a Hamiltonian's disagreement on a batch is refused. `dp` and the control, which may be finite differences of H, are compared above the consistency check's noise floor, taken row by row, about 2.2e-6 at |H| ~ 1. The consistency check's probe nodes add the 3 that `spread_samples` picks (4 in 3-D) to the middle and the end nodes, since on a uniform grid those are where a term such as sin(2 pi x) takes one value; so `validate_hamiltonian_consistency` probes up to 2d + 4 nodes (2d + 5 in 3-D). `refuse_a_hamiltonian_whose_reads_disagree` takes `times=` and `unwalled=`, and reads a Hamiltonian that never ran `MFGOperatorBase.__init__` with the consistency check's step.
