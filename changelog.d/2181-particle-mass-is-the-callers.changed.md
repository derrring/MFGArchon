- **`FPParticleSolver`'s default `kde_normalization` becomes `NONE`, and `KDENormalization.INITIAL_ONLY` is removed** (Issue #2181). Callers who did not name the parameter inherit the default change silently, so it is recorded apart from the fix. **Why `ALL` is not the default:** since the same change measures every slice on `geometry.integrate`'s measure, `ALL` pins exactly what `SolverResult.mass_conservation_error` reports, and that diagnostic reads round-off for any completed solve — measured 2.220446e-16 to 6.661338e-16 across n = 11/21/41 and N = 500/2000/5000. Conservation by fiat is what #1683 removed from `fp_fdm`, `fp_gfdm` and `network_solvers/fp_network`; this solver was the campaign's surviving instance. The same fixtures now report 3.2e-03 to 1.26e-02. `ALL` remains available and is documented as fiat. **Why `INITIAL_ONLY` is gone rather than made the default:** it promised "correct at t=0, then carry", and at t=0 there is no accumulated drift to correct — pinning the calibration slice *is* calibrating on it, so the two are the same arithmetic. Measured `np.array_equal(INITIAL_ONLY, NONE)` is `True` on `reflection`, `cic` and `standard`, against a control `|ALL − NONE|` of 8.0e-03, 7.7e-03 and 4.3e-02; deleting the member outright left 1940 tests green. It appeared to differ only while a defect calibrated the carried factor one slice late, which deleted the t=0 → t=1 KDE transient from every report. A member whose name promises a behaviour that cannot exist is removed rather than aliased, as `TensorProductGrid.volume_element` was in #2157. `ParticleConfig.normalization`'s `Literal` is narrowed to `["none", "all"]` and its default moved with it.
