- **AGENTS.md: a re-review is narrow, and a PR body carries claims, not tallies** (user rulings, 2026-10-13; #2636).
  - § *Review before merge*:
    - Review 1 reads the whole PR.
    - Every later review reads only:
      - each finding of the review before it, with the change that answers it;
      - every file the fix round touched;
      - a regression pass over the PR's own tests and the gate's result.
    - The PR body states what changed, the class line, and the evidence for each claim that gates the merge. Every
      number in it sits beside the command that printed it and the sha it ran at.
    - Measurements no gating claim rests on go in a comment on the PR's issue, which the body links.
    - The one-line record of each review (its number, verdict and tokens) stays in the body.
  - § *Local patches are the anti-pattern* (#2636's S1 and S3):
    - A partial fix's remaining members are listed in an open issue the PR does not close.
    - A staged plan's issue comment records the plan's approval by someone other than its author.
