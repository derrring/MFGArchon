- **Breaking: an input no solve reads is refused when the problem is built** (Issue #2678, #2563). Each was accepted and changed the default solve by 0.0 in `U` and in `M`, with nothing said. Refused now: `Model(drift_field=)`; `MFGProblem(drift=)`, in both forms; `MFGProblem(constraints=)`, in both forms; `state_penalty_scale` other than 1.0 with no `state_penalty`; `suppress_warnings=True` on a geometry or a network, where no feasibility check runs (it stays on a grid the library builds itself, from `spatial_bounds=` or the default domain); and `hamiltonian=` beside `components=`, whose Hamiltonian is the one read. The refusal of a drift names what to do instead: `solve_fp_system(drift_field=)` on `FPFDMSolver` or `FPParticleSolver`, or `FixedPointIterator(drift_field=)` over `FPFDMSolver`. Each replaces the drift the Hamiltonian gives, and reads a callable by position as `(t, x, m)` (#2864). A `Model` now needs a Hamiltonian or a Lagrangian: one with only a `drift_field` built, and then raised at `MFGProblem`.
- **`problem.drift_field` and `MFGProblem.get_drift_coefficient_field` are removed, and `has_state_dependent_coefficients` reads only the volatility** (Issue #2678). No solver read any of them.
