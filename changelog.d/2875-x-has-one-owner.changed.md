- **The `x` a body is handed has one owner** (#2875; the approver's rulings of 2026-11-27). Every door that hands a body its `x` builds it in `types/callable_protocols.py`, through `coordinate_tuple`, `point_tuple`, `point_at` or one of three new functions there: `grid_coordinates` for the points of a tensor grid, `line_coordinates` for points on a line (a jax array under `jit` included), and `moved` for a point moved along one coordinate.
  - **What a body sees.** The same coordinates as before, now as `Coordinates`, a subclass of `tuple`. So `x[0]`, `a, b = x` and `sum(xi**2 for xi in x)` read as before, but `type(x) is tuple` is now false. Where a door used to build a one-point tuple of Python floats, the entries are now numpy floats.
  - **The doors that used to build `x` themselves:** FEM's quadrature points, FVM's faces and its periodic seam, FP-FDM's volatility tensor, the grid a coefficient or a solver's drift is read on (`points_of`), the points HJB refuses as non-finite, a problem source at the density's shape, a boundary value's point, the construction check's probe points, the 1-D Nitsche and adaptive-mesh doors, a Lagrangian's stand-in point in `proximal`, and the 1-D variational and optimal-transport doors.
  - **A binding you build yourself** with `bind_user_callable` refuses, by name, a tuple that its owner did not build, as a defect in the door that handed it. Build `x` with `coordinate_tuple`, `point_tuple` or `line_coordinates`, or pass a tuple of your own through `caller_coordinates`, which keeps its content.
  - **A method you call with an `x` of your own hands it to the body unchanged.** This holds for a Hamiltonian's or a Lagrangian's `dp`, `dm`, `optimal_control`, `proximal` and their dual searches. A point derived from it keeps its form: handed an array `x`, a Hamiltonian's default `dx`, a finite difference, now hands `__call__` arrays, where it built tuples before. The callable that `MFGProblem.volatility` or `MFGProblem.diffusion` returns, a composite drift, and a potential your own call reaches take a tuple of yours as it is, and refuse any other form as before.
  - **The network solvers** still hand a node as `np.array([i])`. The form of `x` on a network is #3012's.
