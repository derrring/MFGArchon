- **AGENTS.md: a code change ordered between reviews owes its reach first** (proposed by the audit session,
  2026-10-20), in § *Branches & PRs*, *Review before merge*. Before the push, whoever orders the change names every
  reader of each value it removes, moves or adds, and the change is measured against main on one case of each input
  class, beside its command in the PR body. A deletion justified by "no case needs it" names the population its
  evidence covered. A defect that predates the PR, found in its review, is filed rather than fixed in a fix round,
  unless its fix is the whole reach of one owner.
