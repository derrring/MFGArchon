- **AGENTS.md: a census's axes come from its sources, a predating finding waits for the next PR, and an evidence
  comment carries what a claim reads** (user ruling, 2026-11-27), in § *Local patches are the anti-pattern*,
  § *A PR stays at its plan* and § *Review before merge*.
  - A census's axes are drawn by script from every source of the values the fact's deciders and readers take in,
    not from the cases found so far.
  - A structural finding present at the base sha goes through *Before filing an issue* by default; the PR takes it
    only when one of its own class lines cannot reach head count 0 without it, and the ruling says so.
  - An evidence comment holds the command, the sha and the output rows a claim reads, a script's source once per
    version, and links rather than restates rulings, plans and earlier comments. A count or a universal is
    written after its command has run, beside it.
