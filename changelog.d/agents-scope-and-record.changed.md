- **AGENTS.md: a census's axes come from its sources, a predating structural finding goes through *Before filing an
  issue* by default, and an evidence comment carries what a claim reads** (user ruling, 2026-11-27), in § *Local
  patches are the anti-pattern*, § *A PR stays at its plan* and § *Review before merge*.
  - A census's axes are drawn by script from every source of the values the fact's deciders and readers take in,
    not from the cases found so far.
  - A structural finding present at the base sha that is not a member of a planned class line goes through *Before
    filing an issue* by default, which gives it a plan of its own. The PR takes it only when the class fix of a
    class line its approved plan names cannot be completed without it. A predating defect found in review is fixed
    in the PR only when the approver admits it so.
  - An evidence comment holds the command, the sha or shas and the output rows a claim reads, a script's source
    once per version, and links rather than restates rulings, plans and earlier comments. In it a count, and in it
    or the PR body a universal claim or a claim of a check, is written after its command has run, beside it.
