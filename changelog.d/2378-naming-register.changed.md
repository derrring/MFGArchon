- **`docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md` § *Names* states the naming register (#2378 item 0.3).** The horizon, the discretisation and the control-cost weight are named by their symbols (`T`, `Nt`, `dt`, `Nx`, `Nx_points`, `lambda_`); configuration and the model's terms are named by descriptive words (`max_iterations`, `relaxation`, `potential`, `volatility`). § *Not settled here* had listed the register as open, but item 0.3 decided it on 2026-09-20. It now lists what the register leaves open instead: whether a scalar coefficient takes a symbol or a word, since the library does both. § *Solver method surface* also records three pre-v0.12 solver-method names that `WeakFormHJBSolver.solve_hjb_system` still accepts silently, including through `MeshlessGalerkinHJBSolver`, which delegates to it (#2429).
