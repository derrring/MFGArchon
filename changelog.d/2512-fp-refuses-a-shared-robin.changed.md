- **A shared ROBIN is refused at every FP solver** (#2512, user ruling 2026-10-10). This is a behaviour change for `FPFEMSolver`, which used to assemble the coefficients of a ROBIN on the problem's shared BC as its own Robin. The other FP solvers refused ROBIN by type before, and now refuse a shared one with this message. On a shared BC, `ROBIN(alpha, beta, g)` is the HJB's `alpha*u + beta*du/dn = g`. An FP Robin condition is on the total flux, `J.n = (D/beta)(alpha*m - g)`, which is not `alpha*m + beta*dm/dn = g` where the drift crosses the wall, so the HJB's coefficients do not say what the FP's are. The refusal names both readings and says how to specify the two equations' BCs separately: keep the ROBIN on the problem for the HJB, and pass the FP solver its own `boundary_conditions=no_flux_bc(...)`. FP-FEM also takes a ROBIN of its own, as the FP's total-flux Robin. A ROBIN read only by an HJB solver is unaffected. The convention is in `docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md`, § "A Robin condition at the FP".
