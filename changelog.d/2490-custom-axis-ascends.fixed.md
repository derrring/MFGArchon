- **A `TensorProductGrid` axis strictly ascends, and the grid owns it** (#2490, the folded #2748). The custom branch admitted an axis in any order and kept the caller's array, while every reader of an axis takes its index order as its coordinate order. On a descending axis a region marked at x = 1 governed `x_min` and the measure's weights summed to −1; on an axis with one swapped pair the weights stayed positive and integrated wrongly, with nothing raised; and reversing the caller's array after construction reversed the grid's axis.
  - `TensorProductGrid(spacing_type="custom", ...)` refuses an axis that descends, swaps or repeats a node, by name: the axis, the first node where the order fails, and the two coordinates there.
  - Each axis, custom or uniform, is the grid's own read-only float64 copy, so a later write into the caller's array, or into the grid's, cannot reorder it.
  - `CartesianGrid` declares `coordinates` and its order. The readers that took it off any geometry now read it only from a `CartesianGrid` and refuse another geometry by name: the region reader, `GridPointMeasure`, `get_spatial_grid`, FP-FDM's node lookup, `ImplicitHeatSolver` and the particle solver's bounds. Where a reader has another path it takes that one: the face cells are cut without node midpoints, and the particle solver's density axes for a geometry that is no grid come from its bounds (#2757).
