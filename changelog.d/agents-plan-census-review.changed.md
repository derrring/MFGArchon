- **AGENTS.md: a plan's census is drawn a second time before approval** (user ruling, 2026-11-28), in § *Local
  patches are the anti-pattern*, item 3. A fresh read-only reviewer, neither the plan's author nor its approver,
  draws it at the base sha and the head, given the plan's statement of the change (the fact, the owner, the symbols
  named at plan) but not its census. Each place or axis it finds goes into the plan, with how the plan treats it,
  before approval, and counts as the plan's. A plan revised after a review, or whose statement of the change
  changes, owes a fresh draw by a new reviewer; writing in the places the second draw found does not. The drawer is
  not one of the PR's three reviews.
