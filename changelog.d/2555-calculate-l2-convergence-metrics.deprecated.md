- **`calculate_l2_convergence_metrics(U_new, U_old, M_new, M_old, Dx, Dt)` is deprecated** (Issue #2555). It measures a sweep's change with one scalar spacing, which § 9 of `docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md` retires. Use `sweep_change(U_map, U_old, M_map, M_old, problem.spatial_measure().integrate, dt)`. The deprecated form calls it on the measure that weighs every node `Dx`, so its values are unchanged to rounding; a test checks them against both.
