  - **Main solves, this change refuses** (the user's ruling of 2026-11-26):
    - `GraphMFGSolver`, both of whose solvers carry their own BC, with two declarations that differ or a FLUX on the shared BC. Graph never asked the owner.
    - A BC changed on the problem between two solves of one iterator, so that its declarations differ, is refused at the next solve. At main FixedPoint refused it, reading the owner every sweep. FictitiousPlay, both Block iterators, Newton, Graph, RegimeSwitching and MultiPopulation solved it.
  - **Main refuses, this change solves** (the approver's ruling of 2026-11-26): `FixedPointIterator` whose two solvers carry their own BC, with the problem's BC changed during one solve, from an iteration callback. At main FixedPoint's per-sweep read refused it at the next sweep; the read is now once per solve. Where a solver reads the owner itself, that solver refuses it at the next sweep, at main and here. Stage 2a's driver is to read the owner at every sweep on every route (#3002).
  - **The callers of the provider check this release removes (#2424), and of FixedPoint's per-sweep resolution,** each read the owner; the reads in `IterationState` keep their refusals.
