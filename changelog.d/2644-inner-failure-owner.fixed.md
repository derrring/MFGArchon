- **An inner nonlinear iteration that exhausts its budget no longer hands back its last iterate as the answer** (Issue #2644, #2673).
  - **One owner:** `base_hjb.report_inner_solve_failure`. Every member hands its failure there:
    - HJB-FDM's 1-D Newton, its nD Newton and value iteration;
    - the weak-form Newton (HJB-FEM, meshless Galerkin);
    - HJB-GFDM's Newton;
    - Howard's policy iteration, standalone and inside HJB-GFDM;
    - network policy iteration.
  - **The policy:** by default the owner raises `ConvergenceError`. Under `on_inner_failure="record"` it keeps the iterate, records the step in `inner_solve_failures()`, and warns with a `RuntimeWarning`; before, HJB-GFDM and the weak-form Newton wrote a log line no warning filter or result could see, and Howard and network policy iteration said nothing.
  - **Budgets:** a budget below 1 is refused by every solver, the weak form's Newton among them, which took no Newton step and handed each time step's starting iterate back as its answer. The module-level Newton step keeps a budget of 0, which measures and reports its starting iterate (#1878).
- **A coupled solve's HJB solvers record for its sweeps, and every coupler reads the record at its verdict** (#1878): the fixed point, both block iterators, fictitious play, multi-population, regime switching, the graph solver and Newton. Before, only the fixed point read it, so a `BlockIterator` over failed inner steps reported `converged=True`.
  - Each slot's solver is read by its `inner_solve_failures()`, its latest solve, which is the last sweep's. One HJB solver object in several slots, which made its latest solve only its last slot's, is refused before the solve (see the `.changed` fragment).
  - Every coupler's result lists the last sweep's failed inner steps, converged or not: `metadata["inner_hjb_failures"]`, and on Newton's result, a tuple, `info["inner_hjb_failures"]`. The deprecated `return_tuple=True` tuple of the fixed point and fictitious play carries no metadata. A refused verdict names the failures in its `convergence_reason`.
  - Newton's reason names them too, where it said "Newton max iterations". It also says how Newton stopped: on a named stop (line_search_failed, diverged) the last verdict read the iterate it returns, and when the budget ran out, the iterate before it.
