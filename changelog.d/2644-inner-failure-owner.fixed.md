- **An inner nonlinear iteration that exhausts its budget no longer hands back its last iterate as the answer** (Issue #2644, #2673).
  - **One owner:** `base_hjb.report_inner_solve_failure`. Every member hands its failure there:
    - HJB-FDM's 1-D Newton, its nD Newton and value iteration;
    - the weak-form Newton (HJB-FEM, meshless Galerkin);
    - HJB-GFDM's Newton;
    - Howard's policy iteration, standalone and inside HJB-GFDM;
    - network policy iteration.
  - **The policy:** by default the owner raises `ConvergenceError`. Under `on_inner_failure="record"` it keeps the iterate, records the step in `inner_solve_failures()`, and warns with a `RuntimeWarning`; before, HJB-GFDM and the weak-form Newton wrote a log line no warning filter or result could see, and Howard and network policy iteration said nothing.
  - **Budgets:** a budget below 1 is refused by every member, the weak form's Newton among them, which took no Newton step and handed each time step's starting iterate back as its answer.
- **A coupled solve's HJB solvers record for its sweeps, and every coupler reads the record at its verdict** (#1878): the fixed point, both block iterators, fictitious play, multi-population, regime switching, the graph solver and Newton. Before, only the fixed point read it, so a `BlockIterator` over failed inner steps reported `converged=True`.
  - The record read is the last sweep's, every solve of it. A solver's `inner_solve_failures()` is its report, and a solver in one slot is read by it. A coupler can give one HJB solver object several nodes or regimes, as `GraphMFGSolver([p, p], ..., [h, h], ...)` does, and that object's report is then only its last slot's, so its solves are read one by one from a per-solve log of that report. Where they cannot be (an object that logs fewer of its solves than the slots it fills, or reports something other than its last logged solve), the result is not reported converged, and `metadata["convergence_reason"]` says the solves "cannot be read one by one".
  - Every coupler's result lists the last sweep's failed inner steps, converged or not: `metadata["inner_hjb_failures"]`, and on Newton's result, a tuple, `info["inner_hjb_failures"]`. A refused verdict names them in its `convergence_reason`.
  - Newton's reason names them too, where it said "Newton max iterations", and says its last verdict read the iterate before the one it returns.
