- **The meshless Galerkin pair places Dirichlet conditions on the faces the BC's face owner gives them** (#2490; #2512, convention row 5, and row B1 stage B1c-2, whose entry states the placement rules). **Behaviour change:** `region_name` face labels, layered priorities and `boundary="all"`, which solved wrongly, raised or were refused, now solve as the owner places them; inputs the owner refuses are refused at construction.
  - **`sdf_region` is no longer integrated on its zero level set.** The path did that, the curved-boundary design of #1139. `BCSegment` documents `sdf_region` as selecting the part of the boundary where it is negative. For a ball of radius 0.2 centred on a corner of the unit square, the conditions landed on a quarter circle inside the domain (7 quadrature points at the path's 16-cell floor, none on the boundary); for an SDF selecting a whole face (`1 − x`) the level set is the face but its normal points inward (FP mass ratio 0.9293, where the face alone gives 0.8326). That branch and the now-unused `surface_quadrature` are removed; #1139 records how to restore them under their own semantics.
  - **Fixed: `region_name="x_max"` is now the face x_max.** It was imposed on every face (0.6652); it now solves exactly as `boundary="x_max"` (0.8326).
  - **Fixed: `boundary="all"` names every face** (#1953). A NO_FLUX `"all"` segment with a DIRICHLET default constructs again (#2523 refused it). A Dirichlet `"all"` segment, which raised at assembly, is imposed on every face it governs.
  - **Fixed: precedence.** A face named by several segments is the one the owner's order gives it. A priority -1 Dirichlet `"all"` under a priority 1 Dirichlet x_max holds each face at its own segment's value; imposing both on x_max had averaged them.
  - **The translator alone decides absorbing.** The meshless FP built its Nitsche terms without the value (`include_data=False`), deciding Dirichlet → absorbing a second time. Both solvers now assemble the data load from the BC they hold. The FP holds the shared BC read through `fp_view_of_shared_bc`, where a Dirichlet value is 0, so it still absorbs, bit-identically. The `include_data` parameter is gone.
  - The BC guide's NEUMANN note said a nonzero Neumann value is "silently ignored by every FP solver". Every grid FP solver now refuses it at construction (FDM, FVM, GFDM, SL and particle since #1686, FEM since #2294, the meshless pair since #2523), and the note says so.
  - **A zero the translator accepts is a zero here.** The Nitsche data reader takes its "verifiably zero" from `bc_utils._describe_bc_value`, so `np.int64(0)`, `np.zeros(1)` and `None` absorb on the FP, bit-identically to main; on the HJB, where main raised, they impose 0. Any real scalar or size-1 real array is read as a value; a complex one is refused (`float()` would drop its imaginary part).
