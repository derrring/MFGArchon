- **A 2-D potential written per point, such as `x[0]**2 + x[1]**2`, is evaluated per point again** (Issue #2714). `SeparableHamiltonian` probed the potential with two points to decide whether it takes a batch, so in 2-D a per-point potential that is elementwise in the coordinates it indexes returned one value per coordinate, passed as one value per point, and was cached as vectorised: a wrong `V` on two-point batches and a broadcast error on larger ones, which 2-D `HJBFDMSolver` reported as a Newton convergence failure and 2-D `HJBGFDMSolver` as a `ValueError`. The probe now has `d + 1` points, so a first batch of one point also decides by probing rather than falling back to per-point evaluation for good.
