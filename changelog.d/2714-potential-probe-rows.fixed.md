- **A per-point potential in 2-D is evaluated per point again** (Issue #2714). `SeparableHamiltonian` probed the potential with two points to decide whether it takes a batch, so in 2-D a potential written per point (`x[0]**2 + x[1]**2`) returned one value per coordinate, passed as one value per point, and was cached as vectorised: a wrong `V` on two-point batches and a broadcast error on larger ones, which a 2-D `HJBFDMSolver` solve reported as a Newton convergence failure. The probe now has `d + 1` points.
