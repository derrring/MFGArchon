- **`ImplicitDomain.segment_intervals(a, b)` and `first_contact(a, b)`: the exact parts of a segment inside an implicit domain, and its first boundary contact** (#2733, PR 1). A `Hyperrectangle` solves a slab test, a `Hypersphere` a quadratic, and the CSG nodes combine their children's intervals, a complement entering as the closed set phi >= 0. Each interval end carries the primitive and face that bound it, except where it is the segment's own end, and `first_contact` gives the contact's parameter, point and outward normal. Two walls that are one wall in floating point, such as a union's shared face at 0.3 and 0.1 + 0.2, leave a gap between two intervals no thicker than 4 ulps across both walls; where `contains` holds at its midpoint, the two intervals are joined (#2733 PR 2). Any other gap is outside, including one through two obstacles that touch at a point. Whether a start is inside is decided by the intervals at t = 0, which agree with `contains` there, not by a band around phi = 0. A domain given only by its signed distance is refused by name rather than sphere-traced, since nothing checks that its phi is a distance, and so is a periodic box (#2704). The particle solver's contact loop reads `first_contact` (#2733 PR 2).
