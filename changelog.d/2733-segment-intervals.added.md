- **`ImplicitDomain.segment_intervals(a, b)` and `first_contact(a, b)`: the exact parts of a segment inside an implicit domain, and its first boundary contact** (#2733, PR 1). A `Hyperrectangle` solves a slab test, a `Hypersphere` a quadratic, and the CSG nodes combine their children's intervals, a complement entering as the closed set phi >= 0. Each interval end carries the primitive and face that bound it, except where it is the segment's own end, and `first_contact` gives the contact's parameter, point and outward normal. Two surfaces that are one wall in floating point, such as a union's shared face at 0.3 and 0.1 + 0.2, or a ball's end at 0.65 - 0.35, leave each leaf's interval ending at its own surface; such a seam is joined where `contains` holds across it: a gap at most 1024 coarsest ulps wide on every axis, the coarsest ulp being the largest spacing of its ends' coordinates, is read at points evenly spaced in its parameter, at its midpoint and at each double of an axis that holds at most 1024, and joined where all hold (#2733 PR 2). The reads and the gap's computed ends lie on the segment's lattice a + t (b - a), whose spacing is about the larger of that ulp and ulp(t) |b - a|: a feature narrower than that spacing between reads can be crossed, and a seam reflects where its computed gap is wider than the gate. Measured away from the origin, a one-ulp seam reflected only above |tan phi| = 1024 to its normal, and cracks of two ulps or more reflected at every angle below that. Within about |b - a| / 1000 of the origin both give way: a crack 5e-19 wide at 1e-5 was crossed on 360 of the 491 steps of 0.05 whose intervals showed it, and a seam at 1e-5 reflected from |tan phi| = 206; and a large coordinate on an axis the step does not move along coarsens the gate (#2794). A crack narrower than the parameter resolves can reach the join with its two ends one or two doubles of t apart, or be merged by the union before it. Whether a start is inside is decided by the intervals at t = 0, which agree with `contains` there, not by a band around phi = 0. A domain given only by its signed distance is refused by name rather than sphere-traced, since nothing checks that its phi is a distance, and so is a periodic box (#2704). The particle solver's contact loop reads `first_contact` (#2733 PR 2).
