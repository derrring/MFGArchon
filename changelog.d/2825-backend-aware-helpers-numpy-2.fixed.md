- **`backend_aware_zeros_like`, `backend_aware_ones_like` and `backend_aware_assign` no longer read a NumPy array as a torch tensor** (Issue #2825). NumPy >= 2 arrays have `.device`, which the three helpers took to mean "a torch tensor": the first two sent an ndarray to `torch.zeros_like` / `torch.ones_like` and raised, and `backend_aware_assign` skipped converting an ndarray value into an MPS tensor and raised. All three now ask one owner, `isinstance` against `torch.Tensor` when torch is loaded, which never imports torch and keeps a tensor subclass defined outside torch on the torch branch.
