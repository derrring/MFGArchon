- **The Legendre pairing now carries `-p·α`, so `α* = -∂H/∂p` holds for every Lagrangian** (#2375 ruling 5, #2378 phase 3).
  - `H(x, p, m, t) = sup_α { -p·α - L }` and its inverse `L(x, α, m, t) = sup_p { -p·α - H }`. `H` is `L*(-p)`, not `L*(p)`.
  - **Nothing in this library changes value.** Every concrete control cost shipped here is even in α — `QuadraticControlCost`, `L1ControlCost`, `BoundedControlCost` on a symmetric domain, and the Moreau–Yosida regularisation all satisfy `L(a) == L(-a)` to 1e-14 — and `sup{-p·α - L}` equals `sup{+p·α - L}` exactly when `L` is even. So this is a correctness change for Lagrangians callers write, not a change to any shipped result. If you use only the built-in costs, nothing moves.
  - **What was wrong.** For a non-even `L` the optimal control came back off by twice the odd part. With `L(a) = 0.5a² + 0.3a` the library returned `α* = -(p - 0.3)` where the control that actually minimises `p·α + L(α)` is `-(p + 0.3)`: at `p = +2`, `-1.7` against a true `-2.3`. The returned value was a well-formed control of plausible magnitude, and the sign convention it came from was correct for every cost in the repository, which is why no test could see it.
  - **Both halves of the pair moved together, and that is the substance of the change rather than a detail.** Flipping `H` alone leaves `L → H → L` returning `L(-α)` — again a well-formed Lagrangian, again equal to `L` for every even `L`, and wrong for exactly the non-even ones the convention was changed for. Verified in closed form (exact, no grid): the round trip recovers `L(α)` only when both sides carry the same sign on the pairing.
  - **Changed:** `DualHamiltonian.__call__` and `_find_optimal_alpha`, `DualLagrangian.__call__` and `_find_optimal_p`, and `LagrangianBase.conjugate_argmax` — three branches each (1-D grid, nD scipy, nD `ImportError` fallback). `conjugate_argmax` keeps its documented contract of returning `∂H/∂p` rather than `α*`; #1642 made it the single extension point precisely so `optimal_control` and `evaluate_hamiltonian` could not fork sign conventions, and that structure is preserved.
  - **Corrected while here:** `DualHamiltonian.dp`'s docstring claimed `∂H/∂p == α*`, which could not hold alongside `HamiltonianBase.optimal_control` returning `-dp`. Under the new pairing `∂H/∂p = -α*`, and both statements now agree.
  - **If you have written a non-even Lagrangian**, your `optimal_control` and `H` values change, and the new ones are the correct ones. A non-even *control cost* subclass must additionally re-derive its closed forms: the analytic `evaluate`/`optimal_control` bodies on `ControlCostBase` subclasses are unchanged, and are correct only because their `L` is even. That caveat is now recorded at the line.
