- **The Legendre pairing now carries `-p·α`, so `α* = -∂H/∂p` holds for every Lagrangian** (#2375 ruling 5, #2378 phase 3).
  - `H(t, x, p, m) = sup_α { -p·α - L }` and its inverse `L(t, x, α, m) = sup_p { -p·α - H }`, in the argument order of #2378 phase 5. `H` is `L*(-p)`, not `L*(p)`.
  - **Nothing in this library changes value.** Every concrete control cost shipped here is even in α — `QuadraticControlCost`, `L1ControlCost`, `BoundedControlCost` and the Moreau–Yosida regularisation all satisfy `L(a) - L(-a) == 0.0` **bit-exactly**, probed across the `ℓ1` kink, the interior and deep saturation — and `sup{-p·α - L}` equals `sup{+p·α - L}` exactly when `L` is even. So this is a correctness change for Lagrangians callers write, not a change to any shipped result. If you use only the built-in costs, nothing moves. **That evenness is now checked** (#2386, same release): `test_every_library_control_cost_is_even` imports every module in the package in a child interpreter, failing if any `.py` file under it was not reached, and for one instance of every concrete `ControlCostBase` subclass it finds asserts `L(a) == L(-a)` bit-exactly at values of α inside the admissible set and that `effective_domain()` is symmetric; the two conjugates agree when both hold. A cost class added later at module level that fails either check fails the test. Not checked: instances built with other constructor arguments, α with more than one component, and classes defined inside functions. Until #2386 the evenness was measured by hand twice and asserted only through its conjugate consequence, at `abs=1e-6`, for three of the four costs.
  - **What was wrong.** For a non-even `L` the optimal control came back off by twice the odd part. With `L(a) = 0.5a² + 0.3a` the library returned `α* = -(p - 0.3)` where the control that actually minimises `p·α + L(α)` is `-(p + 0.3)`: at `p = +2`, `-1.7` against a true `-2.3`. The returned value was a well-formed control of plausible magnitude, and the sign convention it came from was correct for every cost in the repository, which is why no test could see it.
  - **Both halves of the pair moved together, and that is the substance of the change rather than a detail.** Flipping `H` alone leaves `L → H → L` returning `L(-α)` — again a well-formed Lagrangian, again equal to `L` for every even `L`, and wrong for exactly the non-even ones the convention was changed for. Verified in closed form (exact, no grid): the round trip recovers `L(α)` only when both sides carry the same sign on the pairing.
  - **Changed:** `DualHamiltonian.__call__` and `_find_optimal_alpha`, `DualLagrangian.__call__` and `_find_optimal_p`, `LagrangianBase.conjugate_argmax` and `LagrangianBase.evaluate_hamiltonian` — three branches each (1-D grid, nD scipy, nD `ImportError` fallback). `conjugate_argmax` keeps its documented contract of returning `∂H/∂p` rather than `α*`; #1642 made it the single extension point precisely so `optimal_control` and `evaluate_hamiltonian` could not fork sign conventions, and that structure is preserved.
  - **Corrected while here:** `DualHamiltonian.dp`'s docstring claimed `∂H/∂p == α*`, which could not hold alongside `HamiltonianBase.optimal_control` returning `-dp`. Under the new pairing `∂H/∂p = -α*`, and both statements now agree.
  - **The nD initial guess moved with the maximiser.** `scipy` is seeded with `x0 = clip(-p)` rather than `clip(+p)` at five sites. Under the old pairing the maximiser sat near `+p/λ` and the old guess started *on* it; after the flip it sits near `-p/λ`, so an unchanged guess began a full `2p/λ` away. On a convex `L` the local search converges regardless and nothing is observable — but on a non-convex one it settles in the wrong basin: measured on `L = 0.5|a|² + 2Σcos(3a) + 0.3Σa` in 2-D, `H` came back **-5.6891 against a global 7.3497** at one momentum, and 5 of 6 probes missed. With the guess corrected, 1 of 6 misses by 0.41 — better than the 1-of-6-by-0.82 the *old* pairing managed. The residual is a pre-existing hazard this change does not own — the nD `scipy` branch searches locally while its own 1-D and `ImportError` siblings grid globally, so the three branches of one function already disagree on non-convex input — but it is **23% of random momenta, not a corner**, and a 3-seed multistart measures three orders of magnitude better on both a separable and a non-separable probe. Filed as a follow-up rather than done here. Note also that `clip(-p)` is not pointwise dominant: at 19 of 300 momenta the old seed found a better basin, so this is a large average improvement and not a strict one.

  - **If you have written a non-even Lagrangian**, your `optimal_control` and `H` values change, and the new ones are the correct ones. A non-even *control cost* subclass must additionally re-derive its closed forms: the analytic `evaluate`/`optimal_control` bodies on `ControlCostBase` subclasses are unchanged, and are correct only because their `L` is even. That caveat is recorded on `BoundedControlCost.evaluate`, which is one of four such closed forms; `QuadraticControlCost`, `L1ControlCost` and `_MoreauYosidaControlCost` carry the convention statement but not the evenness caveat.
