- **AGENTS.md: a review's checkouts are worktrees, not copies** (user ruling, 2026-11-11), in § *Branches & PRs*, *Review before merge*. Whoever puts a review's base or head on disk takes `git worktree add --detach` or `git archive`, and the session that opened it removes it once the review has returned; recursive copies of the working tree carried their ignored directories and outlived their reviews.
