- **User-facing text no longer calls `MFGProblem(model=..., domain=..., conditions=...)` "the v1.0 API"; no interface is fixed for v1.0 yet.** Error messages, docstrings, the examples, the 2-D tutorial notebook and `docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md` call it the model/domain/conditions form.
  - The legacy constructor's `DeprecationWarning` drops "Use the v1.0 API" and "Legacy support will be removed in v1.0.0", and no longer names a removal version. Its first sentence, which the warning census keys on, is unchanged.
  - The errors that said "API v1.0" or "v1.0 API" now name the form without a version ("model/domain/conditions", or `MFGProblem(model=, domain=, conditions=)` in the missing-argument error). They are: the missing-argument and mixed-argument `ValueError`s; `with_model()`, `with_domain()` and `with_conditions()` on a legacy problem; a solve-time `Nt` override on a legacy problem; and the `sigma=` `TypeError`'s pointer to `Model(volatility=...)`. The `m_initial` refusal on `Conditions` no longer says "v1.0".
