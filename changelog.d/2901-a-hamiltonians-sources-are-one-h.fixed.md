- **A Hamiltonian whose sources disagree is refused at construction** (#2901). The library reads a Hamiltonian through several sources: `__call__` in the HJB residual, `dp` in its Jacobian, `optimal_control` in the Fokker–Planck drift, the doors over them on a batch and on a point, and, for a separable H, its control cost. A problem checked `dp` and `dm` against `__call__` (#1642), and `evaluate_H` and `evaluate_dp` on a batch against a point (#1884), and nothing else. So a `SeparableHamiltonian` subclass whose `optimal_control` was another Hamiltonian's was solved as a mixture with no error: at bfe6de4d, #2901's own reproduction gave `max|M - M_ref| = 7.411e-01`.
  - **The methods at a point.** `validate_hamiltonian_consistency`, with the same witness rule, now also compares `optimal_control` with $-\partial_p H$ where $H$ is smooth, and each door with its method at one point.
    - **Its probes vary every axis.** $t$ is $0$, $T/2$ and $T$; $x$ is the node nearest the domain's centre and each axis's two end nodes; $m$ is $0.5$ and $1$; and $p$ takes both signs on every component, each at a magnitude of its own. Every comparison uses them, the old `dp` and `dm` ones included.
    - **What the old probes missed.** They held $t = 0$, a single $x$, and $p \ge 0$ on the diagonal. At adf83ec2 a control equal to $(1 + 4t)$ times the library's, over the library's `__call__` and `dp`, was accepted, and the default solve returned a density 0.600 off with no error.
    - **A disagreement between the probes is not seen.**
    - **One reader.** Every check reads each source through one reader, as its readers call it, and reads its values whatever array holds them: an H of shape $(1,)$ at a point used to be compared at no probe, and a `dp` returned as a $(1, d)$ row was refused. A source that returns another number of values is refused at the first probe where it does so. A door is compared with its method read the same way, so a staticmethod or property control behind a doubled door is refused.
    - **Each comparison has its own guard.** One that cannot be evaluated at a probe is warned about, and the others are still compared. H that cannot be evaluated at any probe is refused, and so is a method or door that raises at every probe, a point-only Hamiltonian's included, whose doors the batch check does not read.
    - **Cost.** On `DualHamiltonian`, which solves an optimisation per point, the check takes about 0.2 s in 1-D and 2-D.
    - **The refusal** names the pair that disagrees and the probe.
  - **Each door on a batch.** `refuse_a_hamiltonian_whose_reads_disagree` now reads all four doors on a batch against one point at a time, at $t$ of $0$, $T/2$ and $T$. Before, it read `evaluate_H` and `evaluate_dp` only, at $t = 0$, where a control that reads $x$ as one point is right whenever H's $x$-dependence grows with $t$.
  - **On a `NetworkHamiltonian`.** The rates are compared with `dp` by the network's own identity, and `dp` and `dm` with differences of `__call__`, at a fixed set of nodes: both ends of the node order, a middle node and the node with the most neighbours, at three costates and the uniform density. A disagreement at another node or density is not seen.
  - **The soft wall.** `state_penalty` is read back through the composed Hamiltonian's `__call__`. A subclass whose `__call__` does not read the potential the wall is composed into is refused, where it used to drop the wall silently. The probes include the grid node where the wall is largest, so an interior obstacle between the others is seen. The composed Hamiltonian's doors are compared with its methods too, since the solvers read the composed one: a door written without the potential used to drop the wall.
  - **The library's own Hamiltonians are accepted.**
  - **The cost stands for H only as the library ships both,** for the readers of the cost or of a Lagrangian built on it (user ruling, 2026-11-11). HJB-SL's DPP path, on which a non-smooth separable Hamiltonian is solved, refuses a `SeparableHamiltonian` subclass and a user's cost (a subclass of a shipped one, or a shipped cost whose instance holds its own method, `effective_domain` included), and so do HJB-GFDM's `inner_solver='howard'` and `monotonicity_scheme='positive_qp_fan'`. Each refusal says what was run and on what. Before, a subclass whose `__call__` carried another term was solved without it on the DPP path (1.2 from HJB-FDM's U at bfe6de4d), and a cost redefining only its Lagrangian put Howard 0.33 off Newton. `MFGComponents` derives a Lagrangian from the cost only there, also through a `regularize()` wrapper, so a user's cost the wrapper holds derives none; and the FP route asks the Hamiltonian, not its cost, whether it is smooth.
  - **HJB-SL's `diffusion_method='canonical_cs'`** reads the cost's `lambda_` only for a Hamiltonian and cost as the library ships them (#2914's owner). It refuses a `SeparableHamiltonian` subclass, or a `QuadraticControlCost` subclass, even one that changes nothing, as FP-FDM's −c∇U route does since #2915; before, a subclass that redefined H was solved with the cost's answer. `BaseHJBSolver._control_cost_lambda` reads the same owner. HJB-GFDM's DMP warning reads its drift from the Hamiltonian's `dp`, refused unless it has p's shape.
  - **Removed:**
    - `_control_cost_lambda`'s fallback to `problem.lambda_`, for a problem with no Hamiltonian or a Hamiltonian with no control cost;
    - HJB-SL's no-Hamiltonian branch of its characteristic velocity.

    A problem always carries a Hamiltonian (#670), and nothing but a stand-in in a test reached either.
  - **Stage 1 of #2901.** A `SeparableLagrangian` subclass given alone is still read through its own `__call__` on HJB-SL's DPP path and through its cost by the Hamiltonian derived from it; #2901's stage 2 derives that Hamiltonian only for the Lagrangian as the library ships it.
