- **A Hamiltonian whose sources disagree is refused at construction** (#2901). The library reads a Hamiltonian through several sources: `__call__` in the HJB residual, `dp` in its Jacobian, `optimal_control` in the Fokker–Planck drift, the doors over them on a batch and on a point, and, for a separable H, its control cost. A problem checked `dp` and `dm` against `__call__` (#1642) and nothing else. So a `SeparableHamiltonian` subclass whose `optimal_control` was another Hamiltonian's was solved as a mixture with no error: at bfe6de4d, #2901's own reproduction gave `max|M - M_ref| = 7.411e-01`.
  - **The methods at a point.** `validate_hamiltonian_consistency`, with the same witness rule, now also compares `optimal_control` with $-\partial_p H$ where $H$ is smooth, and each door with its method at one point.
    - **Its probes vary every axis.** $t$ is $0$, $T/2$ and $T$; $x$ is the node nearest the domain's centre and each axis's two end nodes; $m$ is $0.5$ and $1$; and $p$ takes both signs on every component. Every comparison uses them, the old `dp` and `dm` ones included.
    - **What the old probes missed.** They held $t = 0$, a single $x$, and $p \ge 0$ on the diagonal. At adf83ec2 a control equal to $(1 + 4t)$ times the library's, over the library's `__call__` and `dp`, was accepted, and the default solve returned a density 0.600 off with no error.
    - **A disagreement between the probes is not seen.**
    - **A probe that cannot be evaluated** is skipped and warned about, and the others are still compared.
    - **Cost.** On `DualHamiltonian`, which solves an optimisation per point, the check takes about 0.2 s in 1-D and 2-D.
    - **The refusal** names the pair that disagrees and the probe.
  - **Each door on a batch.** `refuse_a_hamiltonian_whose_reads_disagree` now reads all four doors on a batch against one point at a time. Before, it read `evaluate_H` and `evaluate_dp` only.
  - **On a `NetworkHamiltonian`.** The rates are compared with `dp` by the network's own identity at every node. `dp` and `dm` are compared with differences of `__call__` at a fixed set of nodes, each over its neighbourhood.
  - **The soft wall.** `state_penalty` is read back through the composed Hamiltonian's `__call__`. A subclass whose `__call__` does not read the potential the wall is composed into is refused, where it used to drop the wall silently.
  - **The library's own Hamiltonians are accepted.**
  - **The cost stands for H only as the library ships both,** for the readers of the cost or of a Lagrangian built on it (user ruling, 2026-11-11). HJB-SL's DPP path, which a non-smooth Hamiltonian takes, refuses a `SeparableHamiltonian` subclass and a user's cost (a subclass of a shipped one included), and so do HJB-GFDM's `inner_solver='howard'` and `monotonicity_scheme='positive_qp_fan'`. Each refusal says what was run and on what. Before, a subclass whose `__call__` carried another term was solved without it on the DPP path (1.2 from HJB-FDM's U at bfe6de4d), and a cost redefining only its Lagrangian put Howard 0.33 off Newton. `MFGComponents` derives a Lagrangian from the cost only there, and the FP route asks the Hamiltonian, not its cost, whether it is smooth.
  - **HJB-SL's `diffusion_method='canonical_cs'`** reads the cost's `lambda_` only for a Hamiltonian and cost as the library ships them (#2914's owner). It refuses a `SeparableHamiltonian` subclass, or a `QuadraticControlCost` subclass, even one that changes nothing, as FP-FDM's −c∇U route does since #2915; before, a subclass that redefined H was solved with the cost's answer. `BaseHJBSolver._control_cost_lambda` reads the same owner. HJB-GFDM's DMP warning reads its drift from the Hamiltonian's `dp`, refused unless it has p's shape.
  - **Removed:**
    - `_control_cost_lambda`'s fallback to `problem.lambda_`, for a problem with no Hamiltonian or a Hamiltonian with no control cost;
    - HJB-SL's no-Hamiltonian branch of its characteristic velocity.

    A problem always carries a Hamiltonian (#670), and nothing but a stand-in in a test reached either.
