- **A Hamiltonian whose sources disagree is refused at construction** (#2901). The library reads a Hamiltonian through several sources: `__call__` in the HJB residual, `dp` in its Jacobian, `optimal_control` in the Fokker–Planck drift, the doors over them, and, for a separable H, its control cost. A problem checked `dp` and `dm` against `__call__` (#1642) and nothing else, so a `SeparableHamiltonian` subclass whose `optimal_control` was another Hamiltonian's was solved as a mixture with no error: at bfe6de4d, #2901's own reproduction gave `max|M - M_ref| = 7.411e-01`.
  - **Now** `validate_hamiltonian_consistency`, with the same witness rule, also compares `optimal_control` with $-\partial_p H$ where $H$ is smooth, and each door with its method, one point at a time on a Hamiltonian that does not read a batch. Its probes vary $t$ (a problem hands $0$, $T/2$ and $T$) and $x$ (the domain's middle node and each axis's two end nodes) as well as $m$ and $p$, for every comparison it makes, the old `dp` and `dm` ones included: at the single $x$ and $t = 0$ it probed before, a control $(1 + 4t)$ times the library's, over the library's `__call__` and `dp`, was accepted, and the default solve returned a density 0.600 off with no error. A disagreement between the probes is not seen. On `DualHamiltonian`, which solves an optimisation per point, the check takes about 0.2 s in 1-D and 2-D. On a `NetworkHamiltonian` it compares the rates with `dp` by the network's own identity, at node probes. The refusal names the pair that disagrees and the probe. The library's own Hamiltonians are accepted.
  - **HJB-SL's `diffusion_method='canonical_cs'`** reads the cost's `lambda_` only for a Hamiltonian and cost as the library ships them (#2914's owner). It refuses a `SeparableHamiltonian` subclass, or a `QuadraticControlCost` subclass, even one that changes nothing, as FP-FDM's −c∇U route does since #2915; before, a subclass that redefined H was solved with the cost's answer. `BaseHJBSolver._control_cost_lambda` reads the same owner, and HJB-GFDM's DMP warning reads its drift from the Hamiltonian's `dp`.
  - **Removed:** `_control_cost_lambda`'s fallback to `problem.lambda_` when the problem carries no Hamiltonian, and HJB-SL's no-Hamiltonian branch of its characteristic velocity. A problem always carries a Hamiltonian (#670), and nothing but a stand-in in a test reached either.
