- **Two capped-exit tests bound their round-off on the mass scale, so the nightly suite passes on Linux** (Issue #2858). `test_fp_fdm_honours_the_cap_on_its_own_bc` allowed the capped outflow 1e-12 relative to its 5e-7 total, 5e-19, below double precision on an O(1) mass, and failed on Linux at 1.3e-17; the release test's mass closure used a fixed 1e-12 over up to 640 steps and failed at 1.07e-12. Both now bound steps * n * eps * mass and state the bound when they fail. The excess is round-off, not an overshoot: every capped step sits at cap * dt to 0.05 eps * mass. No behaviour changes; a 1e-6 relative overshoot of the cap, or a record 1e-6 short, still fails them.
