- **Two capped-exit tests bound their round-off on the scale it is computed on** (Issue #2858). `test_fp_fdm_honours_the_cap_on_its_own_bc` allowed the capped outflow 1e-12 relative to its 5e-7 total, 5e-19, below double precision on the exit node's row the record is read off, and failed on Linux at 1.3e-17; it now allows steps * eps * mass. The release test's mass closure, the residual of its solves, used a fixed 1e-12 over up to 640 steps and failed on Linux at 1.07e-12; it now allows steps * n * eps * mass, since the solves' residual scale grows with the diffusive CFL, which is proportional to n there. Each assertion states its bound when it fails. The excess is round-off, not an overshoot: the exit is capped at every step, and each step's record is within 0.09 eps * mass of cap * dt. No behaviour changes; a cap overshoot of 1e-8 relative still fails the cap test, and a record 1e-9 short on the steps the cap does not hold fails the release test.
