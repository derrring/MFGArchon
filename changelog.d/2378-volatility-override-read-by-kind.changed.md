- **A per-solve volatility override is `volatility=`, and an array declares its kind (#2378 phase 4 part 2a).**
  Breaking (pre-1.0; deprecation clause 4 waived by the maintainer, 2026-09-28).
  - **Retired.** `volatility_field=`, `volatility_matrix=`, `tensor_diffusion_field=` and `diffusion_field=` on every solver `solve_fp_system` / `solve_hjb_system`, on `solve_fp_nd_full_system` and `solve_hjb_system_backward`, and on the `FixedPointIterator`, `FictitiousPlayIterator`, `BlockIterator`, `NewtonMFGSolver`, `MFGResidual` and `HJBHowardSolver` constructors. Each raises a `TypeError` that names `volatility=`. The refusal is scheduled to go at v0.25.0, and the deprecation audit reports it: `check_internal_deprecation.py --show` lists each refusing function under every name it is reachable by, aliases and inheriting subclasses included, and `docs/user/DEPRECATION_MODERNIZATION_GUIDE.md` has a *Refused parameters* section. On a function that takes `**kwargs` the audit holds it back, because without the refusal the name would be accepted and ignored (#2419).
  - **The kind is declared, never read from the shape.** An array override takes `volatility_kind="field"` (one σ per point) or `"tensor"` (a `(d, d)` Σ, or `(*grid, d, d)` per point), as `MFGProblem` has since part 1. A `(d, d)` tensor and a field on a d x d grid are the same array. A scalar takes no kind. A callable takes none, or `"tensor"` if it returns Σ. A per-axis `(d,)` vector is refused; pass `np.diag(v)` with `volatility_kind="tensor"`.
  - **`MFGProblem.solve()` no longer forwards the problem's volatility.** Each solver reads the problem's own volatility by its declared kind, under the rule an override follows, and every override is read by one owner, `resolve_volatility_override`. The forwarded object was the reason ten sites decided a kind by checking whether a value *is* `problem.volatility`. Those identity checks are gone, except the one in FP-FDM's strict-adjoint step, which goes with that step's `sigma=` parameter in part 2b. An override equal to the problem's volatility is read like any other override, and the semi-Lagrangian and WENO HJB solvers accept it only when its value and kind equal what they read at construction. Expert Mode with a solver pair built from a problem of another volatility is now refused by the coupling loop's pairing guard; it used to solve at the calling problem's volatility with the other problem's grid and Hamiltonian, which is neither problem.
  - **Refusals that now apply to an override as they already did to the problem's own volatility.** HJB-FDM takes a tensor only as a constant diagonal Σ; a non-diagonal, per-point or callable tensor override is refused, where it used to be truncated to its diagonal or averaged over the grid with a warning. FVM refuses a tensor even when its entries are equal. The strict-adjoint FP step of `BlockIterator` refuses a tensor. A negative scalar override is refused.
  - **What to change in your code.** `volatility_field=` or `diffusion_field=` → `volatility=`; `tensor_diffusion_field=` or `volatility_matrix=` → `volatility=` with `volatility_kind="tensor"`; add `volatility_kind="field"` to an array field. `volatility_kind` comes after every existing positional parameter, so no slot moves -- except in the internal `solve_fp_nd_full_system`, where removing the `tensor_diffusion_field` slot moves the parameters after it by one; call it with keywords.

- **Six defects in how overrides were read are closed** (census on #2378, measured at `31de2179`).
  1. FP-FDM ignored a tensor override when the drift was a callable, and solved at the problem's volatility.
  2. FP-FDM read the same `(d, d)` array as a tensor on the potential route and as a spatial field on the callable-drift route, where it was refused for not matching the grid.
  3. FVM solved an all-equal `(2, 2)` override as the scalar it repeats, and dropped `diffusion_field=` when `volatility_field=` was also given.
  4. HJB-FDM and FP-FDM read the same inputs differently: a 3-D `(N, 3, 3)` field, and a tensor-valued callable.
  5. `FixedPointIterator` took `diffusion_field=` with only a warning.
  6. `examples/basic/diagonal_tensor_mfg.py` passed `tensor_diffusion_field=` to HJB-FDM, which has no such parameter. The example still stops earlier, at its `m_initial`, as it does at `ac177dd4` (#1624).

  Measured against `ac177dd4` with a 33-fixture override oracle: 24 solves are bit-identical; 7 change status, all intended (3 callable-drift tensors now solve, 2 per-axis vectors and 2 tensors now refused); 1 raises the same way; and 1 moves, the tensor-valued callable on the callable-drift route, which now equals the constant Σ it returns bit for bit. The part 1 solve oracle, 123 problem-level fixtures solved through a scheme, an expert pair or an iterator, is unchanged: 55 solves bit-identical, 68 raising as before. The Expert-Mode refusal above is outside both oracles and has its own pin. Pins: `tests/unit/test_alg/test_volatility_override_by_kind_2378.py`, each checked against a mutation that reverts its fix.
