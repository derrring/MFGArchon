- **A refusal of a `region_name` segment advises routes that are read as it says** (Issue #2920). The face rule, the point rule and the point rule's refusal of a geometry that is no grid now take their advice from one owner, by the kind of geometry they name. On a `TensorProductGrid` without the region, they say to mark the region first and then attach the BC with `set_boundary_conditions`: a grid whose axes differ in node layout reads a periodic BC's faces when it is built, before any region can be marked. They had advised `mixed_bc_from_regions(geometry, ...)`, which needs the region marked, raises `TypeError` on a geometry such as `Hyperrectangle`, and keeps a part-face region as a `region_name`, which a reader with no geometry refuses again. On a grid that marks no regions, they advise `sdf_region=` and `region=`, read at points and, for a whole face, by a face-level reader; on a geometry that is no grid, `sdf_region=` and `region=`, read at points; with no geometry, `mixed_bc_from_regions(grid, ...)` for a whole face, or `region=` and `sdf_region=` read at points. `boundary=` is offered only for a region that is a whole face, which it governs whole.
