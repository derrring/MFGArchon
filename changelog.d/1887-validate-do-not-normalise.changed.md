- **`MFGProblem` no longer rescales your initial density; it validates the mass, reports it, and warns** (Issue #1887). The constructor used to divide `m_initial` by its discrete integral, silently. Three tiers replace it, and only the third assumes a target of 1: **refuse** a non-finite density, a negative one, or one whose mass is not positive; **report** the mass together with the NAME of the measure that produced it, as `problem.initial_mass` and `problem.initial_mass_measure`; **warn**, silenceably, when the mass is not 1, naming the number, the measure and the remedy. The measure is named because the branches are different objects rather than fallbacks of one — a network has no cell volume, an unstructured geometry has no quadrature — and a number without its measure recreates the invisible convention the report exists to remove. `mass = 1` is not a law of the Fokker-Planck equation, which conserves whatever it starts with. **Why the rescale had to go:** a miscoded `m_initial` never announced itself, you got a different object back; the convention was invisible, and before #2145 it was `sum(m)*dx` on a grid where that is not the mass; and it made multi-population shares impossible to express — `MultiPopulationProblem` holds `list[MFGProblem]` and the share of population *k* is `∫m_k = θ_k`, carried by the density itself, so two populations built for 0.3 / 0.7 both came out at 1.000000 with the decomposition destroyed (design follow-up #2173). **What this costs, measured:** the initial mass is the coupling-strength multiplier, since `f(m) = c·m`, and the old constructor was setting that multiplier to 1 for everybody. Three instruments saw the same fact — three 2-D capability cells moved UNSUPPORTED → FAIL, which is *upward* (at mass 1 they crashed; at the mass their fixtures specify they run to completion, verified under a shim restoring the old rescale); `fvm_muscl/mass_conservation` left `COUPLING_INERT_BASELINE`, because the rescale had pinned every fixture to mass 1 and the cell could not feel its own coupling; and inner-Newton non-convergence warnings on one fixture went 19 → 12 because that fixture got easier, **not** because #1878 moved. 104 new warning identities across 97 files, three of them inside the library. **What did not change:** `examples/` is byte-identical apart from comments. Dividing a demo's density by its integral is not a formatting change — on the tutorial's own configuration, iterations against initial mass run 0.25→50, 0.50→47, 1.00→82, 2.00→did not converge — so "add a line to silence the warning" would have walked several examples toward a cliff while calling itself a migration. The initial mass is the author's modelling decision, and the remaining warnings are the library working. Known gap: a density with no closed form (a KDE, a histogram, a previous solve) has no v1.0 expression, since `Conditions` is callable-only and `lambda _x: array` is refused by the signature validator rather than accepted; two files in this repository hit that today.
