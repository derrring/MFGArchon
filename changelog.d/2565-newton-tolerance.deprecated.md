- **`NewtonMFGSolver(newton_tolerance=)` is deprecated; pass `solve(absolute_tolerance=)`** (Issue #2565). Until its removal, a passed or assigned `newton_tolerance` still acts: it becomes `absolute_tolerance`, an extra bound beside `tolerance=` on the larger of U's and M's dt-weighted space-time L2 change rather than an unscaled 2-norm, so it can tighten the stop but no longer loosen it. A `DeprecationWarning` says so, and passing both is refused.
