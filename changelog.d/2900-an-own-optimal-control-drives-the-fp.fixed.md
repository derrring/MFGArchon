- **A `SeparableHamiltonian` that serves an optimal control of its own now drives the FP equation with it, or the solve refuses** (Issue #2900). Handed the value function, FP-FDM and the semi-Lagrangian solver formed the drift as $-c\,\nabla u$, the control cost's. So on `problem.solve()`'s default scheme a subclass that redefined $H$ solved with its base class's drift, with no error: 1.2074e+00 off in $M$ from the library `SeparableHamiltonian` for the same $H$, on the fixture of `test_fp_drift_from_optimal_control_2900.py`.
  - **Which Hamiltonians:** every one that does not serve `SeparableHamiltonian.optimal_control` bound to itself, with a `QuadraticControlCost` serving that class's own. An optimal control defined on the class, inherited, wrapped, assigned on the instance, served by a static or class method, a property or `__getattribute__`, or by a `QuadraticControlCost` subclass.
  - **Routed:** FP-FDM takes its `optimal_control` as a velocity where that route has been measured equal to its value-function route: one population on a 1-D grid with no-flux walls, handed the density trajectory, with an advection scheme that reads a velocity. That is the default scheme there, and each node of a `GraphMFGSolver` solve. It solves as the library Hamiltonian it describes.
  - **Refused:** FP-FDM off that set, the semi-Lagrangian solver, HJB-FDM's `build_advection_matrix`, and the meshless HJB's streamline diffusion. Off the set means periodic walls, a 2-D grid, other walls, `FDM_CENTERED`, a `MultiPopulationIterator` solve, or `RegimeSwitchingIterator`, which hands no trajectory. Each refusal states what was run and on what, and why it refuses, and points to CONVENTIONS § "What the Fokker–Planck solver receives", which describes the solvers that read `optimal_control` themselves and what each one refuses. It names no other scheme.
  - **Unchanged:** FVM, the particle solver and the weak-form family read `optimal_control` themselves and keep the value-function route. The particle solver hands it $m$ = `None`, and the weak-form family also $t = 0$ (#2903). Every library Hamiltonian keeps its route.
