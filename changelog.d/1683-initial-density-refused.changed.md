- **A direct FP-FDM solve refuses a negative initial density instead of clipping it** (Issue #1683). `FPFDMSolver.solve_fp_system(M_initial)` bypasses `MFGProblem`'s check on `m_initial`, and its time-stepping loop zeroed every negative entry it was handed: an entry of -0.5 came back as 0.0 with no error and no warning. It now raises `ValueError` naming the minimum, as `MFGProblem` does. The library does not clip on its own; `AGENTS.md` now states the ruling and its one admitted shape, the mass-fabrication gate's round-off clamp, and #2485 classifies the remaining `np.clip` calls.
