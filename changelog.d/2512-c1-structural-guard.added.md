- **A structural guard keeps C1's consumers from measuring a sweep's change, or deciding on one, outside the owners** (#2512, #2555). C1 governs the outer coupling tolerance, which bounds the change one sweep makes, the map's output against its input. `tests/unit/test_alg/test_c1_change_has_one_owner_2512.py` scans everything under `alg/numerical/coupling/`, and any other module of `alg/` that references `PicardConfig`, its `picard` attribute, `BaseCouplingIterator`, `check_convergence_criteria` or `sweep_change`.
  - It fails on a norm, a max-abs or a (weighted) sum of squares of a difference, on `d @ d`, `dot(d, d)` and `vdot(d, d)`, and on what it treats as a convergence verdict outside `check_convergence_criteria`: a `<`, `<=`, `>` or `>=` comparison with exactly one tol-named side, or `allclose` / `isclose` with a tol-named `atol` or `rtol`. A difference may be reached through a name or through `.ravel()`, `.flatten()` or `.reshape()`.
  - It also fails, anywhere under `alg/`, on a call to the deprecated `calculate_l2_convergence_metrics` or to #2566's `calculate_error`, `compute_norm`, `MFGConvergenceChecker` or `create_convergence_checker`.
  - Excluded by qualified name, each pinned: the verdict's owner, and `AndersonAccelerator.update`, whose norms decide how to mix. `NewtonMFGSolver` decides through the owners since #2565 and carries no exclusion.
  - Its controls plant rivals in a copy of the tree: in an iterator, in a new module outside `coupling/`, and beside each exclusion.
  - `docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md` § 9 states the scope, and why `HomotopyContinuation` and `PrimalDualMFGSolver` are outside it.
