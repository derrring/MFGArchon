- **A structural guard holds convention C1 to its owners** (#2512, #2555). `tests/unit/test_alg/test_c1_change_has_one_owner_2512.py` fails if a module that takes the outer coupling tolerance measures a sweep's change itself. Such a module is anything under `alg/numerical/coupling/`, or any other module of `alg/` that references `PicardConfig`, `check_convergence_criteria` or `sweep_change`. The shapes it catches are a norm, a max-abs or a sum of squares of a difference, `d @ d`, `dot(d, d)` and `vdot(d, d)`, including through a name assigned from a difference. It also fails on a call to the deprecated `calculate_error` or `calculate_l2_convergence_metrics` anywhere under `alg/`. Its controls plant a rival in a copy of the tree, inline and through a named difference, and in a new module outside `coupling/`. `docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md` § 9 states the scope, and why `HomotopyContinuation` and `PrimalDualMFGSolver`, which stop on their own tolerances, are outside it. `scripts/oracle_pins.txt` lists the guard under C1, so C1 counts as met again: it has behavioural pins over every current consumer, and a structural guard over the owner.
