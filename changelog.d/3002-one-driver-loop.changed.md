- **One driver loop for the one-component coupled routes** (#3002, stage 2a). `FixedPointIterator`, `FictitiousPlayIterator`, the Block iterators and `NewtonMFGSolver` run in `coupling/coupled_driver.py`. Each builds the driver with its sweep map, its order between the fields and its damping policy, and every evaluation of the map goes through one sequence of checks (the owner read, the HJB guard, the FP-output rule, the measure). Newton runs a warm-up phase, then a Newton phase built from the step `NewtonSolver.solve` is made of.
  - **The constructors are unchanged. `solve` keeps every existing parameter and gains some:**
    - `FixedPointIterator.solve` gains `track_rate_history`.
    - `FictitiousPlayIterator.solve` gains `track_measure_field`.
    - `BlockIterator.solve` and `NewtonMFGSolver.solve` gain `iteration_callback`, `track_measure_field` and `track_rate_history`. Their `max_iterations` default is now `None`, the route's default, where it was 100 and 30.
  - **The budget** (the approver's ruling 2):
    - `max_iterations` counts every sweep of a solve, Newton's warm-up included. 0 runs none, and a negative one is refused. `FixedPointIterator` and `FictitiousPlayIterator` used to read 0 as unset and ran their default budget. On a negative one they raised numpy's `negative dimensions are not allowed`, the Block iterators returned at once, and Newton ran to convergence.
    - `newton_max_iterations` bounds Newton's phase within the budget. Newton used to run its whole warm-up and then its Newton phase whatever the budget: at `picard_warmup=3` and `newton_max_iterations=2` it ran 5 sweeps at budgets 0 and 1 on the measured problem. It now stops at the budget.
  - **A spent budget reads `"Maximum iterations reached"`** on every coupled route. The six fixed-point routes used to give an empty reason, and Newton `"Newton max iterations"`. Newton still gives `"Newton max iterations"` when `newton_max_iterations` runs out with budget to spare, at 0 included.
  - **`FictitiousPlayIterator`'s callback now reads like every other route's** (#2886, the user's ruling 4). Returning `False` stops the solve; returning `True` or `None` continues. It used to be the other way round: `True` stopped and `False` continued. A callback written for the old meaning now does the opposite, so check its return value. Its stop reads `"callback_stopped (...)"`, as on every route, where it read `"Stopped by iteration callback"`.
  - **A Newton warm-up sweep makes one HJB solve and one FP solve.** It used to make two of each, to compute `picard_residuals`, which is removed (the approver's ruling 6).
  - **The records** (the approver's ruling 7): every one-component route takes `iteration_callback` and `track_measure_field`, and every one except FictitiousPlay takes `track_rate_history`. FictitiousPlay keeps its rate record on, as before, without the keyword.
  - **One progress display,** the hierarchical one FixedPoint showed. The Block iterators' and Newton's `print` lines under `verbose` are gone. Newton's warm-up calls its inner solvers as `FixedPointIterator` does, without `show_progress=False`, so their own progress bars show as they do under FixedPoint.
  - **Unchanged, bit for bit, on the measured problems:** every route's result under its defaults, and under a budget it spends. The arrays, the histories and the iteration counts are compared against main at 8598206f (#3002's evidence). These cases change, beyond the ones listed in this release's fixed entries:
    - a Newton warm-up that diverges records the diverged sweep's inner HJB failures, as `FixedPointIterator` does, where it recorded the sweep before;
    - a coupling cost that HJB-FEM cannot evaluate off its grid: Newton used to refuse it at its first warm-up sweep. It now runs its warm-up and refuses at the post-warm-up check, or returns the warm-up's result when the warm-up converges.
