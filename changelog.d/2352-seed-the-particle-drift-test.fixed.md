- **The particle drift test is seeded, and the issue's diagnosis of why it varied was wrong** (Issue #2352).
  - `test_callable_drift_with_array_diffusion` ran unseeded. #2352 recorded the variation as depending on xdist worker distribution. **Measured: it does not, and cannot.** The result is not inherited from whatever ran before it in the worker. Two entropy sources, both traced to source: the ensemble's initial positions come from `RandomState(None)` in `utils/numerical/particle/sampling.py` (fresh OS entropy per call, since the test constructs the solver with no seed), and `tests/conftest.py`'s autouse `cleanup_numpy_state` calls `np.random.seed(None)` after **every** test, re-entropying the global legacy stream that drives the Brownian increments.
  - **`np.random.seed()` cannot seed this test**, which is the trap worth recording: setting a global seed immediately before the body does not reproduce it — five runs at one global seed gave 0.0263, 0.0289, 0.0221, 0.0251, 0.0235. Only the solver's own `seed=` reaches it. Now `FPParticleSolver(problem, num_particles=1000, seed=7)`, verified bit-identical at `0.027120150809` across repeats.
  - **The test's own comment understated the spread by a factor of four.** It cited 30 seeds giving `0.02274..0.02718`. Over **3300 runs** — 3000 unseeded repeats plus 300 distinct solver seeds, statistically identical, means agreeing to six digits — the range is `0.018189..0.031243`, mean 0.024802, sd 0.001938. The band is `(0.018000, 0.032625)`, so the observed minimum clears its lower bound by **1.0%**, not by the margin 30 seeds implied. Zero failures in 3300, so the band is kept; the thin headroom is the argument for pinning the seed rather than widening it, since widening to ±5σ would cut the exclusion margin against the `sigma=0.1` population from 31% to 10%.
  - The corrected figures are struck in place beside the originals, because the 30-seed number is what the band was chosen against and a reader needs to see that it moved.
