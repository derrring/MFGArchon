- **FP-FEM and the meshless FP keep a negative value, and say how large it is, rather than clip it** (Issue #2845, #2485's ruling; user rulings, 2026-11-04). **Breaking.** `WeakFormFPSolver` zeroed every negative DOF, forward and adjoint, which added mass and returned a conserved-looking density. Now the value stays, nothing is added to the mass, and its size goes in one warning per solve and in `result.metadata["fp_negative_density"]`. FP-FDM's gate does not change.
  - **What the report holds:** per time level, the negative part's share of that level's mass and the level's minimum, then their largest, lowest and sum. On the meshless FP these are MLS coefficients, not the density at a point (#2672).
  - **A density read that cannot take a negative now stops,** on every solver, with `ValueError`, naming the read, the time and the minimum of `m`. `coupling`, `coupling_dm`, `congestion_factor`, `congestion_factor_dm`, a `coupling_cost` and a `terminal_coupling`. A body that raises on a negative, or returns a complex, stops the same way with its own message kept. The solve used to end with `converged=False` and `diverged_nan`, whose log named an HJB divergence.
  - **What now stops that converged with the clip:** a `np.log(m)`, `np.log(m + 1e-8)`, `np.sqrt(m)` or `m**1.5` coupling on FP-FEM on a coarse mesh. `np.log(m)`, `problem.solve()`, `Nt = 8`, sigma 0.3: 81 and 289 nodes stop, and 1089 and 4225 converge, as they did with the clip. A floor in the callable changes the equation being solved, and its size decides whether it helps: on these rows `np.log(np.maximum(m, 1e-4))` converged at 81 and 289 nodes, and `1e-10` did not.
  - **What newly stops on another solver:** `np.log(m)` where a solver returns an exact zero. On FP-FDM and the particle FP with a compact `m_initial`, a coupled run that ended at `converged=False` with no non-finite in `U` now stops at the read, naming a minimum of 0.
  - **What it does not catch:** a read that stays finite, such as `1/m`. Only the warning covers it.
  - **What the clip was hiding:** on `problem.solve()`, `Nt = 8`, smooth data, the mass drift at sigma 0.3 and 81 nodes goes from +3.7e-3 to round-off, and the density's minimum goes from about 0 to -2e-2. At sigma 0.1, each of 289 to 66049 nodes goes negative past 1e-8 of a level's mass, and no finer mesh has finished; #2888 holds the scheme's side.
  - **`refuse_what_a_flux_takes_negative` keeps its logic, and its text now states its measure,** the mass by which the flux takes the density negative, with no mention of clipping.
  - **`FixedPointIterator`'s output validation no longer checks a density this path reports itself.** Its advice was to make the density non-negative, which is the user's to choose.
  - **A subclass may clip:** one whose `solve_fp_system` clips what it returns has run, and it is the caller's change of scheme (`docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md` § *The measure*).
