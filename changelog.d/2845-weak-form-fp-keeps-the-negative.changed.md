- **FP-FEM and the meshless FP keep a negative value, and say how large it is, rather than clip it** (Issue #2845, #2485's ruling; user rulings, 2026-11-04). **Breaking.** `WeakFormFPSolver` zeroed every negative DOF, forward and adjoint, which added mass and returned a conserved-looking density. Now the value stays and nothing is added to the mass. FP-FDM's gate does not change.
  - **What the report holds:** per time level, the negative part against that level's positive part and the level's minimum, then their largest, lowest and sum, in `result.metadata["fp_negative_density"]`, with one logged warning per FP solver. On the meshless FP these are MLS coefficients, not the density at a point (#2672).
  - **A non-finite value function ends a coupled solve with a line that says what was measured:** the site, the iteration and the minimum of the density that solve read. It replaces the guessed "source: HJB (Newton divergence)". It returns `converged=False` and does not raise, so a caller who does not read `converged` holds a `U` with NaN in it; a direct `H(t, x, p, m)` returns what the body returns.
  - **What now ends that converged with the clip:** a `np.log(m)`, `np.log(m + 1e-8)`, `np.sqrt(m)` or `m**1.5` coupling on FP-FEM on a coarse mesh. `np.log(m)`, `problem.solve()`, `Nt = 8`, sigma 0.3: 81 and 289 nodes end at `diverged_nan`, and 1089 and 4225 converge, as they did with the clip. A floor in the callable changes the equation being solved, and its size decides whether it helps: on these rows `np.log(np.maximum(m, 1e-4))` converged at 81 and 289 nodes, and `1e-10` blew up. A scalar-only body such as `math.log(m)` raises its own exception there, and the warning reports the last FP solve.
  - **Its limits.** A read that stays finite, such as `1/m`, is not caught, and only the warning covers it. A `source_term_fp` that goes non-finite is named only by `FixedPointIterator`: the other six couplers raise "a sweep's change must be finite to judge convergence" (#2578), with no read named. The report can accompany that `ValueError` and is not guaranteed to, since it depends on the body as well as the coupler: `GraphMFGSolver` with `0*np.log(m)` as the source logged two lines, and with `coupling=m` added, none. A level that is all NaN holds no negative in the report.
  - **What the clip was hiding:** on `problem.solve()`, `Nt = 8`, smooth data, the mass drift at sigma 0.3 and 81 nodes goes from +3.7e-3 to round-off, and the density's minimum goes from about 0 to -2e-2. At sigma 0.1, each of 289 to 66049 nodes goes negative past 1e-8 of a level's positive mass, and no finer mesh has finished; #2888 holds the scheme's side.
  - **`refuse_what_a_flux_takes_negative` keeps its logic, and its text now states its measure,** the mass by which the flux takes the density negative, with no mention of clipping.
  - **`FixedPointIterator`'s output validation no longer checks a density this path reports itself.** Its advice was to make the density non-negative, which is the user's to choose. It stays on for other solvers.
  - **A subclass may clip:** one whose `solve_fp_system` clips what it returns has run, and it is the caller's change of scheme (`docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md` § *The measure*).
