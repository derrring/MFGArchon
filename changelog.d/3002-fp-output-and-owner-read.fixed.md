- **An FP output a coupled solve cannot use stops it on every route, at every evaluation that reaches a measure or a verdict** (#2997's folded item; #3002, the user's ruling 3). A non-finite one stops with `'diverged_nan'`. One where some time level's mass of |m| passes 1e4 times level 0's stops with `'diverged_mass_blowup'`. The mass is the problem's own measure, `problem.spatial_measure()`, taken at each time level on the route's own invariant: the total for Graph and RegimeSwitching, each population's for MultiPopulation. One rule, `fp_output_stop`, decides it on all seven routes, in Newton's warm-up, its post-warm-up check and its Newton phase alike.
  - **What a stopped solve returns:** the value function the stopped evaluation's HJB solve produced, as U, and that evaluation's FP output, its bad values kept, as M. This is what the HJB guard's stop already returns (#1718). `FixedPointIterator` used to return the damped iterate. `mass_conservation_error` reports None, not measured, for a non-finite M, where FixedPoint's stop reported nan.
  - **Before,** only `FixedPointIterator` stopped, and only when the bad value was not at level 0. On a non-finite FP output the other six routes, and FixedPoint at level 0, raised `ValueError: a sweep's change must be finite to judge convergence`. On a finite but blown-up one:
    - FictitiousPlay, the Block iterators, RegimeSwitching and MultiPopulation ran their budget out;
    - Graph's FP solve raised its refusal of a negative density;
    - Newton reported `'Newton stopped: line_search_failed'`.
  - **A healthy solve with many time levels no longer stops.** `FixedPointIterator` compared the sum of |M| over every time level with the sum over level 0, so a solve that conserved its mass stopped once Nt + 1 passed about 1e4.
  - **The warning names what it measured,** the level's mass and its ratio to the initial mass. It no longer advises stabilisation.
- **A coupled route refuses a solver whose signature `inspect` cannot read, when the route is built** (#2209; #3002 class line 5, the user's ruling). The refusal names the solver, its half (HJB or FP) and inspect's error. Every route reads its solvers' `solve_hjb_system` and `solve_fp_system` signatures through one reader, `solver_signature`, and hands the value function, the problem's source and a volatility override by the keywords they show.
  - **Before,** the routes ran such a solver and solved a different problem without a word. An FP solver was handed U positionally, into its second parameter, which `FPFDMSolver` reads as a drift, so U acted as a velocity rather than a potential. It was handed neither the problem's composed FP source nor the iterator's volatility override, and an HJB solver was not handed the override either. A caller's `drift_field` reached it on `FictitiousPlayIterator` only. `NewtonMFGSolver` already refused such a solver, with inspect's own message.
  - **The 17 concrete solver classes in this release (9 HJB, 8 FP) all have a readable signature,** so the refusal reaches a solver of your own whose solve method `inspect` cannot read, such as a builtin or an extension function.
- **A boundary condition changed during one solve is refused at the next step on every coupled route** (#3005; #3002 stage 2a). The problem then declares two BCs that differ; on the routes that take an iteration callback, a BC changed from the callback is a typical case. Every route reads the problem's BC from its owner before each step: Newton's post-warm-up check and its Newton phase are included (`IterationState.read_owner`).
  - **Before,** every route completed such a solve when its HJB solver carried its own BC. Re-reading the owner was left to the solvers: with both solvers carrying their own BC, or with only HJB-GFDM carrying one, no solver re-read it, because FP-FDM reads it once, when it is built.
- **`NewtonMFGSolver` no longer reports the cold start's mass as measured** (#1717; #3002 class line 6). With no warm-up sweep, Newton's first iterate is the cold start, and a stop there returns it: a named stop such as a failed line search, the HJB guard, or a callback. It is not a density the solve produced, so `mass_conservation_error` now reports None there. On the named stop and the HJB guard it used to report 0.0. A density is counted as measured once a warm-up sweep or a Newton step has produced it.
- **A coupled route hands its solvers copies of the arrays it reads after the call** (#3002 class line 7). That covers the density, the value function, u(T), m(0) and every array keyword, on every route and in every phase, Newton's finite-difference probes and line search included (`base_mfg.handed`). A solver of your own may write into what it is handed.
  - **Before,** such a solver changed the solve's result on every coupled route. A solver that wrote into its FP input emptied the route's own m(0), so the next solve refused a mass of 0.0. On Newton at 8598206f it raised: `NonFiniteReadError` from HJB-FDM's inner Newton step when the HJB solver wrote, a sparse factorization failure when the FP solver did.
  - **Unchanged:** the result for a solver that does not write into its input, bit for bit on the measured problems.
