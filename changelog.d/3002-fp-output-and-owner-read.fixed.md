- **A coupled solve stops on an iterate it cannot use, on every route and in every phase** (#2997's folded item; #3002, the user's ruling 3 and the user's ruling of 2026-11-27). One rule, `FPOutputRule`, reads the FP output of every evaluation of the sweep map, on all eight routes (`FixedPointIterator`, `FictitiousPlayIterator`, the two Block iterators, `NewtonMFGSolver`, `GraphMFGSolver`, `RegimeSwitchingIterator` and `MultiPopulationIterator`; this release's route counts count the Block iterators as two), in Newton's warm-up, its post-warm-up check and its Newton phase alike. The FP solves inside Newton's finite-difference probes and line-search trials, which feed its Jacobian and its Armijo test, are outside it. A second rule reads the iterate every update leaves.
  - **A non-finite FP output** stops the solve with `'diverged_nan'`.
  - **An FP output whose mass grows past 1e4 times the solve's first** stops it with `'diverged_mass_blowup'`. The figure is read on the mass the route's model conserves, the one its `mass_conservation_error` measures. On `MultiPopulationIterator`, whose populations exchange no mass, and on the one-component routes, it is each component's largest level mass of |m| over the time levels, in the problem's own measure, `problem.spatial_measure()`. On `GraphMFGSolver` and `RegimeSwitchingIterator`, whose nodes or regimes exchange mass, it is one figure: the largest, over the time levels, of the sum over the components of the level's mass of |m|. A node or regime can start near empty and fill from another, and is not read as a blow-up. From a solve's second evaluation on, each figure is compared with the same figure in that solve's first FP output. When a figure's first value is 0, or the measure cannot read it, that figure's check is off for the solve, and a warning says so.
  - **An update that leaves a non-finite iterate,** a damping's (a schedule, or Anderson) or a Newton step's, stops the solve with `'diverged_nan'`. With a damping schedule that is inf at sweep 0 and a tolerance of 1e12, six routes used to report `converged=True` over a non-finite U: FictitiousPlay, the Block iterators, Graph, RegimeSwitching and MultiPopulation. Newton's FP solve raised its refusal of a non-finite potential, and only `FixedPointIterator` stopped.
  - **A non-finite warm start is refused by `set_warm_start_data`,** naming the field and the count of non-finite values. A budget of 0 publishes the start as it is. Before, at a budget of 0 the Block iterators returned it with `"Maximum iterations reached"`. Otherwise the solve raised a refusal of the non-finite value where it was read (HJB-FDM's or FP-FDM's `NonFiniteReadError`, or the verdict's `ValueError` of #2578), or, on `FixedPointIterator` with the NaN in U, stopped with `'diverged_nan'`.
  - **What a stopped solve returns:** U is the value function the stopped evaluation's HJB solve produced, and M is that evaluation's FP output, its bad values kept. After an update, the stop returns the iterate the update left, as it is. The HJB guard's stop already returned its HJB output this way (#1718). `FixedPointIterator` used to return the damped iterate. `mass_conservation_error` reports None, not measured, for a non-finite M, where FixedPoint's stop reported nan.
  - **Before, on a non-finite or blown-up FP output,** only `FixedPointIterator` stopped, and only when the bad value was not at level 0. On a non-finite FP output the other seven routes, and FixedPoint at level 0, raised `ValueError: a sweep's change must be finite to judge convergence`. On a finite but blown-up one, on the measured problem:
    - FictitiousPlay, the Block iterators, RegimeSwitching and MultiPopulation ran their budget out;
    - Graph's FP solve raised its refusal of a negative density;
    - Newton reported `'Newton stopped: line_search_failed'`.
  - **Solves `FixedPointIterator` stopped, which it now runs.** FixedPoint compared the sum of |M| over every time level with the sum over level 0 of the density it was handed, at every sweep, the first included.
    - **A source or an inflow that fills the domain.** With m(0) of mass 1e-5 and an FP source adding mass 1 per unit time, FixedPoint stopped at sweep 1. No route stops on it now.
    - **A healthy solve with many time levels.** The sum over Nt + 1 levels passed 1e4 times level 0's once Nt + 1 passed about 1e4.
    - **A growth under 1e4 over the first sweep's output.** An FP output 3e3 times the first sweep's stopped FixedPoint at sweep 2, since the threshold on the sum was about 1e4 / (Nt + 1) per level.
    - **A first FP output already large.** An output 1e5 times FP-FDM's at every sweep, the first included, stopped FixedPoint at sweep 1. It is now its own reference.
  - **Solves no route stopped, which the magnitude now stops.** An FP output that grows past 1e4 times the solve's first, on the seven routes that never read the magnitude, and on FixedPoint from a first output below m(0)'s mass. One 1e-4 times FP-FDM's at sweep 1, then 10 times, now stops every route at sweep 2. On the measured problem it ran six routes' budget out, Newton reported `'Newton max iterations'`, and Graph's FP solve raised its refusal of a negative density.
  - **The warnings name what they measured:** the component, or the components' total, its largest level mass, and its ratio to the solve's first output's. They no longer advise stabilisation.
- **A coupled route refuses, when it is built, a solver whose signature it cannot use to hand the value function** (#2209; #3002 class line 5, the user's rulings of 2026-11-26 and 2026-11-27). Two cases are refused. The refusal names the solver, its half (HJB or FP), and what was read.
  - **A signature `inspect` cannot read.**
  - **An FP signature that names neither `potential_field` nor `drift_field`,** a `(*args, **kwargs)` wrapper's included. Naming the parameter that takes U `potential_field` is what the refusal advises.
  - **Every route reads its solvers' signatures through one reader, `solver_signature`, except Graph's HJB hand-off,** which reads none. The routes hand the value function, the problem's source and a volatility override by the keywords the signature shows.
  - **Before,** every route but `RegimeSwitchingIterator`, which refused it for taking no `source_term`, handed an FP solver whose signature names neither keyword U positionally, into its second parameter. A `(*args, **kwargs)` wrapper over FP-FDM passed U on to `FPFDMSolver`'s second parameter, which it reads as a drift, so U acted as a velocity rather than a potential, and the solve returned a different M from FP-FDM's own without a word.
  - **BREAKING for an FP solver of your own that takes U as its second positional parameter and reads it as the potential.** On the measured problem it returned FP-FDM's own result on those seven routes. It is now refused too. Naming that parameter `potential_field` is the change it needs.
  - **Before, a solver whose signature could not be read** was handed neither the problem's composed FP source nor the iterator's volatility override, and an HJB solver was not handed the override either. On the measured problem, which has neither, HJB-FDM behind such a signature returned HJB-FDM's own result on six routes; it is now refused too (Graph's HJB hand-off reads no signature and still runs it). A caller's `drift_field` reached it on `FictitiousPlayIterator` only. `NewtonMFGSolver` already refused a signature it could not read, with inspect's own message.
  - **The 17 concrete solver classes in this release (9 HJB, 8 FP) all have a readable signature,** and the 8 FP solvers all name `potential_field`. So both refusals reach only a solver of your own.
- **A boundary condition changed during one solve is refused at the next step on every coupled route** (#3005; #3002 stage 2a). The problem then declares two BCs that differ; on the routes that take an iteration callback, a BC changed from the callback is a typical case.
  - **Where the owner is read:** every route reads the problem's BC from its owner (`IterationState.read_owner`) before each step. That includes Newton's post-warm-up check and each iterate of its Newton phase. Newton also reads it before each Newton step, so the step's finite-difference probes and line-search trials run under the owner, and before the F it evaluates when its budget is spent.
  - **Before,** every route completed such a solve when its HJB solver carried its own BC. Re-reading the owner was left to the solvers: with both solvers carrying their own BC, or with only HJB-GFDM carrying one, no solver re-read it, because FP-FDM reads it once, when it is built.
- **Whether a coupled solve's `mass_conservation_error` is a measurement is decided in one place, `DensityRecord`** (#1717; #3002 class line 6).
  - **What counts as measured:** a density that an FP output the stop published, a fixed-point update, or a Newton step produced. The start, cold or warm, does not count.
  - **`NewtonMFGSolver` no longer reports the cold start's mass as measured.** With no warm-up sweep, Newton's first iterate is the cold start, and a stop there returns it: a named stop such as a failed line search, the HJB guard, or a callback. `mass_conservation_error` now reports None there. On the named stop and the HJB guard it used to report 0.0.
  - **`MultiPopulationIterator` reports each finite population's drift** when a stop publishes another population's non-finite density. The non-finite population's drift is None, and so is the top-level figure, the worst over populations.
- **A coupled route hands its solvers copies of the arrays it reads after the call** (#3002 class line 7), through one helper, `base_mfg.call_handed`.
  - **What it covers:** every solver method a route calls with an array, on every route and in every phase. That includes:
    - the density, the value function, u(T), m(0) and every array keyword;
    - Newton's finite-difference probes and line search;
    - Block's `adjoint_mode` calls, `build_linearized_operator` and `solve_fp_step_adjoint_mode`;
    - the density a result's negative-density report reads.

    A solver of your own may write into what it is handed.
  - **Before,** such a solver changed the solve's result on every coupled route. A solver that wrote into its FP input emptied the route's own m(0), so the next solve refused a mass of 0.0. On Newton at 8598206f it raised: `NonFiniteReadError` from HJB-FDM's inner Newton step when the HJB solver wrote, a sparse factorization failure when the FP solver did.
  - **Unchanged:** the result for a solver that does not write into its input, bit for bit on the measured problems.
