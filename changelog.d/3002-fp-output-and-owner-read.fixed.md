- **An FP output a coupled solve cannot use stops it on every route** (#2997's folded item; #3002, the user's ruling 3). A non-finite one stops with `'diverged_nan'`, and one whose mass passes 1e4 times level 0's with `'diverged_mass_blowup'`, measured on each route's own invariant: the total for Graph and RegimeSwitching, each population's for MultiPopulation. One rule, `fp_output_stop`, decides it on all seven routes.
  - **Before,** only `FixedPointIterator` stopped. On a non-finite FP output the six others raised `ValueError: a sweep's change must be finite`. On a blown-up but finite one, FictitiousPlay, the Block iterators, RegimeSwitching and MultiPopulation ran their budget out, Graph's FP raised its refusal of a negative density, and Newton reported `'Newton converged'`.
  - **The magnitude check on FixedPoint is unchanged.**
- **A boundary condition changed during one solve is refused at the next step on every coupled route** (#3005; #3002 stage 2a). A typical case is a BC changed from an iteration callback, so that the problem declares two that differ. Every route reads the problem's BC from its owner before each step, Newton's phase included (`IterationState.read_owner`).
  - **Before,** every route completed such a solve when its HJB solver carried its own BC. With both solvers carrying their own, or with only HJB-GFDM carrying it, no solver re-read the owner; FP-FDM reads it once, when built.
