- **`KDENormalization.INITIAL_ONLY` is removed** (Issue #2181). **BREAKING:** `KDENormalization.INITIAL_ONLY` now raises `AttributeError`, `KDENormalization("initial_only")` raises `ValueError`, and `ParticleConfig(normalization="initial_only")` raises `ValidationError: Input should be 'none' or 'all'`. It promised "correct the reconstruction's drift at t=0, then carry one factor" — and at t=0 there is no accumulated drift to correct, while pinning the calibration slice *is* calibrating on it. The two are the same arithmetic. Measured after #2181's accounting change: `np.array_equal` against `NONE` is **True** on `reflection`, `cic` and `standard`, on the 1-D grid-drift, 1-D callable-drift, 2-D grid-drift, 2-D callable-drift and torch paths, at `Nt == 1`, and with a degenerate first reconstruction — against a live control `|ALL − NONE|` of 8.007e-03, 7.694e-03 and 4.300e-02 on the same runs. Deleting its dispatch branch outright changed no test outcome. It appeared to mean something only while a defect calibrated the carried factor one slice late, which deleted the t=0 → t=1 reconstruction transient from every report. A member whose name promises a behaviour that cannot exist is removed rather than aliased, as `TensorProductGrid.volume_element` was in #2157. **Migration:** pass `KDENormalization.NONE`, which is what `INITIAL_ONLY` did.
