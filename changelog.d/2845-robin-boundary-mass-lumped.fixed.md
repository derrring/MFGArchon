- **P1 FEM lumps a Robin wall's boundary mass as it lumps the time mass, HJB-FEM and FP-FEM together** (Issue #2845). The consistent boundary mass, D(alpha/beta) int phi_i phi_j, has the off-diagonal D(alpha/beta)|e|/6 > 0 on a 2-D boundary edge e, so FP-FEM's implicit step stopped being an M-matrix step where (alpha/beta)|e| > 3 cot(theta_e). On a 33 x 33 init_tensor mesh at dt = 1e-4 and alpha/beta = 97 and 200, the step had 256 positive off-diagonals, and a one-node spike on the wall went to -1.375e-4 and -1.327e-2, which the positivity clip then filled with mass. Lumped, the boundary mass is diagonal: with alpha/beta >= 0 it only adds to the diagonal, the step has no positive off-diagonal, and the spike stays non-negative. alpha/beta < 0 lowers the diagonal and is outside this. The Robin load, the 1-D Robin wall, P2 and alpha = 0 walls are bit for bit as before. On HJB-FEM's 2-D Robin oracle, against main at 16, 32 and 64 cells, the error rises by 13.2%, 17.0% and 20.3% (Picard) and 14.2%, 17.6% and 20.5% (Newton), with refinement ratios 3.55 and 3.63 (Picard) and 3.54 and 3.64 (Newton); FP-FEM's moves by at most 1.1%.
