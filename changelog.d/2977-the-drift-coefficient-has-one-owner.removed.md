- **`MFGProblem(coupling_coefficient=)` and `MFGProblem(lambda_=)`** (#2977), with the attributes `problem.coupling_coefficient` and `problem.lambda_`, the two keys in the records `solve(Nt=)` and the `with_*` rebuilds replay, and #2678's refusal of `lambda_=` on a grid. `lambda_` had no reader. `coupling_coefficient` decided nothing under a quadratic `SeparableHamiltonian`, whose FP drift's coefficient is 1/λ, the Hamiltonian's control cost's, whatever it held. Under any other Hamiltonian it was the drift's coefficient where a solver formed −c·∇U itself, a value no Hamiltonian decided (default 0.5); those solvers now refuse such a Hamiltonian (see Changed). The control cost goes on the Hamiltonian: `QuadraticControlCost(control_cost=)`, or `QuadraticMFGHamiltonian(coupling_coefficient=c)` with c > 0, which sets λ = 1/c; c ≤ 0 is refused (#2980).
- **The drift-coefficient override** of `FPFDMSolver.build_advection_operator(U, coupling_coefficient=)` and `HJBFDMSolver.build_advection_matrix(U, coupling_coefficient=)`. Each builder reads the coefficient from the Hamiltonian, through `fp_drift_coefficient`.
- **`FluxDiagnostics.compute_boundary_flux`'s velocity from `u`**, with its `coupling_coefficient` parameter. Hand it `velocity_field`. The path computed α = −∇u / coupling, the reciprocal of the drift scale its docstring stated.
