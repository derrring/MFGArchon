- **The FDM HJB's upwind momentum has one owner and two presets; Engquist-Osher is the default** (Issue #2313).
  - **Owner:** `upwind_momentum` and `upwind_momentum_derivatives` in `operators/stencils/finite_difference.py` are the one statement of the upwind rule. The 1-D residual, both 1-D Jacobians, the nD residual, `build_linearized_operator` and `build_advection_matrix` all read them. The analytic Jacobian no longer reconstructs the Rouy-Tourin branch by value; it combines the backward and forward difference operators with the owner's derivatives.
  - **Presets:** `HJBFDMSolver(numerical_hamiltonian="engquist_osher" | "rouy_tourin")`, read when `advection_scheme="gradient_upwind"`. Both evaluate H at a per-axis magnitude of the one-sided differences: Engquist-Osher at sqrt(a+^2 + b-^2) (ACD's Example 1), Rouy-Tourin at max(a+, -b-). They differ only at a discrete local maximum along an axis. Neither is called "Godunov".
  - **Why Engquist-Osher:** on the interior its linearisation is the transpose of the FDM FP `divergence_upwind` operator at local maxima as well as elsewhere. Measured on the library's own assembly, max|A_fp - J^T| over interior rows and columns is 2.8e-14 in 1-D and 4.4e-14 in 2-D, against 18.5 and 32.4 for Rouy-Tourin. The wall rows are outside that claim: `build_linearized_operator` zeroes them, which leaves 94.6 (1-D) and 60.4 (2-D) for both presets (#2338). Engquist-Osher is also C^1 wherever H is, so the analytic Jacobian is the exact derivative for a smooth H; for an H with a kink of its own, such as `L1ControlCost`, it is a Clarke element there, as before.
  - **Behaviour change:** default results move wherever the value function has a discrete local maximum along an axis. On the capability matrix's 2-D smoke cells the presets agree to 3.3e-15, because that fixture's U has no interior maximum along either axis.
  - **`build_advection_matrix` moves with the preset, and its "velocity" is not the optimal control at a maximum; its docstring now says so.** At a discrete maximum the Engquist-Osher momentum is up to sqrt(2) times either one-sided difference, a bound reached only where the two tie exactly. Measured at 8debbbbc on the 9x8 state of `test_the_linearised_operator_is_the_derivative_of_the_hamiltonian_it_linearises` (`u = 0.3 cos(4 pi x) cos(2 pi y + 0.3) + 0.11 y + 0.07 x`): the per-node momentum ratio peaks at 1.3252 over 9 of the 42 interior nodes and hits sqrt(2) at none, the matrix's largest entry grows 28.244 to 35.359 (+25.2%), and max|A_EO - A_RT| is 7.864. The mismatch between this operator's velocity and the FP's face differences is pre-existing; this widens it. `BlockIterator`'s velocity modes and `FPFDMSolver.solve_fp_step_adjoint_mode` consume it; the strict-adjoint mode uses `build_linearized_operator` instead.
  - **`gradient_upwind`** is the Rouy-Tourin preset and is byte-identical to before. `GradientOperator` gains `scheme="backward"` and `"forward"`.
