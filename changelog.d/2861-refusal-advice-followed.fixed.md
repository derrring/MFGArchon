- **A refusal's advice on a callable reads right when followed, in the order the callable is written** (Issue #2861). Three refusals advised a rewrite that read a callable written time last with its arguments swapped, with no error: `bind_user_callable`'s refusal of an unnamed callable with `*args` under a reordered role and its refusal of a misplaced name under a role never reordered, both "name its parameters t, x" (13 role and branch pairs), and `adapt_ic_callable`'s refusal of a `functools.partial` that fixes time, "pass lambda x: f(0.25, x)", which read `(x, t)`-written `f = 100 t + x` as 300.25 for 28.0. The binders now advise naming each parameter after what it reads and ordering them by slot. The partial's refusal now writes its lambda by keyword from the names `f` reads, and writes no lambda where they cannot be read or more than one parameter is left. `refuse_methods_out_of_order` no longer says "time first" for `terminal_cost_k` and `initial_density_k`, which take no time.
