- **The semi-Lagrangian HJB solver reads time-dependent boundary data at the step's time (#2453).** The gradient's ghost cells used the gradient operator's default time, t = 0, on the operator-splitting and stochastic paths. The post-step enforcement of sub-steps, of every n-D stochastic step and of the n-D DPP step passed no time either. Measured on a time-dependent Neumann value, 1-D ADI read 54 of its 64 values at t = 0, and 2-D ADI, 2-D stochastic and 2-D DPP read all of theirs there. Each read now uses the step's time, as the 1-D whole-step enforcement already did. For boundary data that does not depend on t, U is byte-identical on nine paths.
