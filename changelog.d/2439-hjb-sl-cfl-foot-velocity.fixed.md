- **The semi-Lagrangian HJB half sizes its sub-steps by the speed its foot moves at (#2439).** The CFL number was `max|grad u| * dt / dx`, but the departure foot moves at `dH/dp`, which is `p / lambda` for the quadratic control cost. So for `lambda < 1` each sub-step crossed `1 / lambda` times more cells than the schedule allowed: 3.3 cells at `lambda = 0.25`, where #1880's fixture then ended in the NaN #2438 describes with no cap involved. It now measures the CFL number on `_characteristic_foot_velocity`, the solver's single owner of `dH/dp`: crossings are at most 0.9 cells, and `lambda = 0.25` and `0.5` stay finite and symmetric. Output is byte-identical at `lambda = 1` in seven configurations (1-D `adi`, `explicit`, `none`, `stochastic` and cubic interpolation; 2-D `adi` and `stochastic`). For `lambda > 1` the half now takes fewer sub-steps, as the foot is slower: 23 against 40 at `lambda = 2` on one fixture.
