- **The agents' SDE volatility has one name, `volatility`, in every public API (#2378 phase 4 part 2b).**
  Breaking (pre-1.0; deprecation clause 4 waived by the maintainer, ruling 16 of 2026-09-28).
  - **`sigma=` is refused, naming `volatility=`, where it meant the volatility.** The functions: the adjoint builders `create_adjoint_consistent_bc_1d` and `compute_adjoint_consistent_bc_values` (in both `alg.numerical.adjoint` and `geometry.boundary`), `build_diffusion_matrix`, `build_diffusion_matrix_1d`, `build_diffusion_matrix_2d` and `build_diffusion_matrix_from_geometry`, `AdjointConsistentProvider`, `DiffusionOperator.from_volatility`, `VariationalMFGProblem`, `create_quadratic_variational_mfg`, `create_obstacle_variational_mfg`, `diffusion_from_volatility`, `diffusion_from_volatility_torch`, `check_adi_compatibility`, and the strict-adjoint step `solve_fp_step_adjoint_mode` on FP-FDM and the weak-form FP solver. The manufactured-solution sources `hjb_source` and `fp_source` also refuse `sigma_kind=`, naming `volatility_kind=`. The refusal goes at v0.25.0, and the deprecation audit lists it.
  - **The internal helpers are renamed too, without a refusal**, because they sit on assembly paths called once per row: the FP-FDM stencil and boundary-row builders, the time-stepping steps, the HJB residual and Jacobian assemblers, the semi-Lagrangian and ADI diffusion steps, the Brownian increments and the Crank–Nicolson helpers. A stale `sigma=` there raises Python's own `TypeError`.
  - **Renamed attributes, fields and keys.**
    - `VariationalMFGProblem.sigma`, `AdjointConsistentProvider.sigma` and `HamiltonianValues.sigma` are now `.volatility`. Reading `.sigma` raises an `AttributeError` naming the replacement.
    - `MFGGridConfig.sigma` is now `volatility`. `MFGGridConfig(sigma=...)` is refused, because this model ignores unknown fields: without the refusal, `sigma=0.3` would have been dropped and the default 0.1 used.
    - The boundary-provider state carries the volatility under `"volatility"`. A state holding `"sigma"` is refused by name, and the deprecated `"diffusion"` key keeps its own schedule (#1512).
    - `save_experiment_data` writes `"volatility"` where it wrote `"sigma"`. Files saved before this carry the old key.
  - **The strict-adjoint FP step reads the volatility by its declared kind.** It used to refuse the problem's tensor by checking `is problem.volatility`, so an equal tensor passed as an override was read as a per-point field. That was the last identity check from part 2a. `BlockIterator` passes the kind.
  - **Docs.** Twenty parameter docstrings called this `sigma` a "Diffusion coefficient"; they now say it is the SDE volatility, with D = σ²/2. So do `OperatorConfig`'s field, the notebook reporter's label and two unread `jax_utils` parameters. Two user guides passed σ as `diffusion=` or labelled it a diffusion, and `AGENTS.md`'s boundary-condition example uses `volatility=`.
  - **Still spelt `sigma`, and not the agents' volatility:** the common-noise processes' own volatility, the Lévy jump width, and the maze Gaussian blur width. A test pins that list.
  - **The refusal decorators return the function they wrap, typed as itself.** Part 2a's `retired_volatility_keywords` was untyped, so mypy read each function it decorated as `Any` and stopped checking the calls to it. That hid 21 errors in `alg`, and part 2a recorded their disappearance as an improvement: the real count went from 762 to 758, not to 737. With the decorators typed, `alg` is at 758, and this part adds no errors.
  - **What to change in your code:** `sigma=` → `volatility=`, `sigma_kind=` → `volatility_kind=`, `.sigma` → `.volatility`, and `state["sigma"]` → `state["volatility"]`.
