- **A Hamiltonian whose `optimal_control` is not -dp at the probes is refused at construction** (#2901, stage 1c-i). The HJB solvers read `__call__` and `dp` and the FP solvers read `optimal_control`, and before this nothing compared the control, so a subclass whose control was another Hamiltonian's was solved as a mixture with no error. `validate_hamiltonian_consistency` now holds the control to -dH/dp beside `dp` and `dm`, at t = 0, T/2 and T, at up to 2d + 1 grid nodes, and at momenta whose components take both signs and magnitudes of their own (docs/user/CONVENTIONS.md, "What construction compares"); a disagreement between or beyond the probes is not seen. Each comparison has its own guard: a source that cannot be evaluated at a probe is warned about and skipped there, and H, `dp` or the control that cannot be evaluated at any probe is refused. What each source returns goes through the owner of its shape: `checked_control` for `dp` and the control, and the new `checked_value` for the value and `dm`, one value per point. So a value or a `dm` of shape (1,) at a point, and a control of shape (1, d), are refused at construction. `validate_custom_functions` takes `optimal_control=` and `times=`.
