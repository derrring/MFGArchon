That included a hand-built pair with the HJB half's sub-stepping off and `FPSLSolver`'s default on, which converges in 60 sweeps before #1880's fix and not after (asymmetry 0.95 after 200), and #1880's own mismatch in the other direction. It now returns `NOT_DUAL` with a warning for such a pair, so Expert Mode says so.
  - Only the switch is compared. On #1880's fixture an FP half at `cfl_target` 0.45 is as stable as the matched pair. With `cfl_target` bounded to (0, 1] (#2448), the switch is the whole of the schedule's structure.
  - Not compared: an HJB half on the canonical-CS or DPP path, for which no oracle says which FP schedule pairs with it (#2441), and a half that declares no schedule.
  - `create_paired_solvers` builds matched pairs as before. Given an `fp_config` that undoes the match, it builds the pair as asked and warns, where it was silent. It also stops writing its defaults into the caller's `hjb_config` / `fp_config`: a reused `fp_config` carried one HJB half's schedule into the next pair.
